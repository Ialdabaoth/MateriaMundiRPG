# obx-core changelog

## 0.1.0 (2026-11-08)
- New root module `obx-core`, `provides: "obx"`, under the OGL 1.0a.
- Tables extracted from the B/X SRD's Open Game Content by `tools/extract_srd.py`: ability modifiers, prime requisite XP, seven classes' level progressions (XP, HD, THAC0, attack bonus, five saves, spell slots), thief skills, turning, weapons and armour.
- Named values for sheet layouts in `derive.json` (SHEET_LAYOUT.md).
- `text/README.md`: the rules Heph will word for 0.1.0.
