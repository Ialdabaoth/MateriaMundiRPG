# Ability scores

Source: E9 System Reference Handbook (© 2026 Bakagaijin Productions), section "What Abilities Do". Heph's own text: the first sentence of each description, verbatim, at his request (2026-11-13).

Strength is your character’s raw physical power, including their muscle strength and speed.

Constitution is your character’s toughness, endurance, and health.

Dexterity is your character’s hand-eye coordination, balance, and agility (although some facets of agility are a matter of strength rather than dexterity).

Intelligence is your character’s logical thinking and deduction capability.

Wisdom is your character’s intuitive awareness and common sense.

Charisma is your character’s courage, spirit, and likeability.
