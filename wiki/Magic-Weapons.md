## Magic Weapons

Enchanted weapons are by far the most straightforward of magic items to use. Since all weapon enchantments require that the base weapon be a masterwork, a magic weapon always provides a +1 to its attack throw, unless its enchantment provides a better bonus. Most magic weapons add an additional die of damage per rarity level, although some weapons provide a more complex attack enhancement instead.

### Aetheric Blade

*Rare (Divine or Primal)*  

*Ingredients: Aether Pearl, feysilver, silver*

An aetheric blade appears to be a finely crafted silver dagger. Upon closer inspection, thin runes and sigils are etched along the blade and the hilt, and inlaid with a metal of a slightly different color.

An aetheric blade completely bypasses armor when it strikes, and inflicts the wielder’s choice of piercing or aetheric damage.

Additionally, as their action the wielder may make a slash in midair, rending open a “hole” in reality that opens into faerie or shadow. The hole remains open until the end of its wielder’s next turn, and the dagger becomes inert until the next sunrise or sunset.

Placing an aetheric blade into a bag of holding is an infamous and absurdly stupid way to improvise a weapon of mass destruction. The magics of both systems prove instantly and fatally incompatible, tearing a hole into the deep aether that sucks in all objects and creatures within 10 paces of the rip. Both magic items, and everything else that falls into the hole, are permanently lost. Creatures may attempt save vs. Hold to avoid being sucked in, which is hard if adjacent to the tear, at normal difficulty 2-5 paces away, and easy if 6 or more paces away. Anyone holding either the bag or the blade as the two items touch automatically fails this saving throw.

### Cannon

*Rare (Arcane)*  

*Ingredients: orichalc, iron*

A cannon is a siege weapon similar to a giant firearm, which typically uses an alchemical reaction to propel an iron ball at extreme velocities. These cannon deal structure damage on a hit, and an equal amount of thunder hit point damage to all creatures with hit points within 10 feet of the point of impact. Cannon are typically mounted on a large carriage mount, which is pulled by horses or mules until it is detached and readied for fire. Aiming, firing, and reloading a cannon each take the combined actions of a full crew of engineers. The minimum crew size depends on the size of the cannon – light cannon require 2 crew, medium cannon require 4, and heavy cannon require 6.

| Cannon Type | Damage | Range Ammo |  |
|---|---|---|---|
| Light Cannon | [1D6+2] | 100 | 20 |
| Medium Cannon | [2D6+1] | 100 | 15 |
| Heavy Cannon | [3D6] | 100 | 10 |

Legendary cannon use a dragon pearl instead of alchemical ammunition; sunlance cannon use flame pearls to inflict radiant structure damage on a hit and flame hit point damage to anything within 10 feet of the point of impact, while stormlance cannon use air pearls to inflict lightning damage on a hit and thunder hit point damage within 10 feet.

### Earthbreaker

*Any Rarity (Arcane or Primal)*  

*Ingredients: Earth Pearl, orichalc, iron*

An earthbreaker is an oversized bludgeon enchanted with elemental Earth energy, adding +1d6 per rarity level to its blunt damage whenever a melee attack with it hits. Additionally, whenever you hit or miss with a melee attack with the weapon, the area in the target’s space becomes difficult terrain.

Against a target with structure points, an earthbreaker deals [1D6 + rarity] structure damage, and inflicts a critical hit on a damage die roll of '6'.

### Firearm

*Uncommon+ (Arcane)*  

| | |
|---|---|
| *Ingredients:* | *orichalc, iron* |

Firearms are ranged crossbow-class weapons which use alchemical charges to fire small pellets of iron or lead at extreme velocities. They are considered simple weapons, usable by anyone that could fire a crossbow of the same size. Each type of firearm is a different level of arcane artifact:

| Firearm Rarity Range Size & Properties |  |  |  |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pistol | U | 15 | Light |
| Musket | U | 25 | Medium |
| Blunderbuss | U | Cone 3 Heavy |  |
| Revolver | R | 15 | Light, Magazine 6 |
| Rifle | R | 50 | Medium |
| Hand Mortar | R | Cone 3 | Heavy (fire damage) |
| Thunderlance | L | 50 | Heavy, Polearm |
| Thunder Rattler |  | L Line 20 Heavy, Magazine 10 |  |

Firearms are a new and volatile technology, and as such bring their own unique set of weapon properties.

**Improved Damage** - All firearms deal full piercing damage for a ranged weapon of their size, plus an equal amount of thunder elemental damage. (Hand mortars deal fire damage + thunder damage, instead of piercing + thunder). Heavy firearms deal [1D6-1] structure damage when aimed at a target with structure points.

**Improved Critical Hit Chance** - All firearms achieve a critical hit whenever any damage die rolls a '6'.

**Misfire** - A firearm misfires and jams when the unmodified d20 attack throw is less than the firearm’s artifact Rarity level. Thus, a pistol or musket jams when the d20 roll is ‘1’, while a revolver or rifle jams on a '1' or '2', and a thunderlance or thunder rattler jams if the d20 roll is ‘3’ or less. You may attempt to unjam a misfired firearm as an action by making a successful Finesse test with tinker’s tools vs. a difficulty based on the firearm’s artifact rarity level - easy for a common firearm, average for an uncommon firearm, or hard for a rare or legendary firearm. On success, the weapon is ready to be used again; on failure, the firearm is broken and must be repaired as an exploration action outside of combat.

**Line / Cone** - A firearm with a range of line or cone does not make an attack roll, instead creating an area of effect that many paces long. Each creature within the area of effect saves vs. Blast.

**Reloading -** A firearm requires an action and an easy Finesse test to reload when empty.

Most firearms are single-shot, and must be reloaded each time they are fired.

**Magazine -** If a firearm has the magazine property, it has an internal store of multiple rounds; one action can refill the entire magazine. A wielder with a Dexterity of +1 or better can fire a firearm with the magazine property a total number of times in one round up to their Dexterity.

| Ammunition | *Common (Arcane)* |
|---|---|
| *Ingredients:* | *Flame and Earth materia residue,* |

*lead and copper*

Firearm ammunition is crafted from lead, brass, and an alchemical formula composed of Flame, Air, and Earth materia residue. The brass ‘jacket’ holds the alchemical charge and the lead ‘bullet’, which is fired out of the barrel at extreme velocity when the charge is ignited. Typically, a firearm uses some kind of striking force to impact a sliver of Flame materia on the back of the jacket to ignite the charge. The ‘jacket’ is typically left inside the firearm, and must be carefully extracted when the firearm is reloaded. A jacket from an expended round will typically be extremely hot during the round immediately after it is fired; failing the Finesse test to reload during this time will inflict 1d6 fire damage to the wielder.

Crafting ammunition for a firearm involves stamping a new brass jacket, mixing the alchemical charge, and casting a new lead bullet. Treat this process as brewing a single alchemical formula at the same rarity as the firearm; when finished, the alchemist rolls 2d6 to determine how many usable rounds are produced.

### Flametongue

*Any Rarity (Arcane or Primal)*  

*Ingredients: Flame Pearl, orichalc, iron*

A flametongue is a longsword or greatsword enchanted with elemental Fire energy. It adds 1d6 worth of fire damage per rarity level whenever a melee attack with it hits, inflicting ongoing fire damage if any damage die rolls a ‘6’. A flametongue may also be used to parry any elemental (cold) attack, including ranged or area attacks; when used to parry an area attack, your saving throw is easier.

### Flying Blade

*Any Rarity (Arcane or Primal)*  

*Ingredients: Air Pearl, feysilver, iron*

A flying blade is a dagger, chakram, or other light throwing weapon enchanted with elemental Air energy. Any light throwing weapon may be made into a flying blade.

A flying blade adds its Rarity level as a static bonus to its to-hit and damage rolls when used to make a ranged attack. Additionally, the blade will return to its thrower’s hand from up to 50 paces away at the end of each combat round. If the blade is being actively restrained from returning by another creature, that creature must make a Strength check to hold onto it, vs. a threshold equal to twice the blade’s rarity.

Legendary flying blades may be guided to attack when not in the wielder’s hands, so long as the wielder has psychically claimed the blade and bonded themselves to it with their blood. Attacking with a blade without throwing it makes the attack throw harder, but might allow the attacker to catch the target unaware.

### Frostbrand

*Any Rarity (Arcane or Primal)*  

*Ingredients: Water Pearl, orichalc, iron*

A frostbrand is a longsword or greatsword enchanted with elemental Water energy. It adds 1d6 worth of cold damage per rarity level whenever a melee attack with it hits, stunning and hobbling the target if any damage die rolls a ‘6’. A frostbrand may also be used to parry any elemental (fire) attack, including ranged or area attacks; when used to parry an area attack, your saving throw is easier.

### Keen Blade

*Any Rarity (Arcane)*  

*Ingredients: Earth Pearl, feysilver, iron*

A keen blade is a magically sharpened and balanced edged weapon, engraved with runes and sigils along the blade and pommel. Any non-blunt weapon may be made into a keen blade, with a rarity based on the size of the blade; light keen blades are common magic items, medium keen blades are uncommon, and heavy keen blades are rare.

A keen blade deals 1d6 weapon damage per rarity level regardless of its wielder's strength, and always inflicts a critical hit if any damage dice roll a ‘6’. The save vs. Doom difficulty to resist a keen blade's critical hit is based on the number of dice that roll a '6', and critical hits always inflict maiming injuries.

### Luckcatcher

*Legendary (Primal)*  

*Ingredients: Aether Pearl, feysilver, iron, silver*

A luckcatcher is a dagger with a cage in the pommel fashioned from cold-wrought iron and silver filigree, and silver runes along the blade’s edge.

Each sunrise and sunset, roll a d20 and record the result. Until the next sunrise or sunset, whenever you attack with this weapon, make a saving throw, or force another creature to make a saving throw, you may swap one d20 result from that throw with the stored d20 result, storing the replaced roll in the luckcatcher.

### Moonblade

*Rare (Primal)*  

*Ingredients: Dream Pearl, feysilver, iron, silver*

A moonblade is a blade or axe fashioned from cold-wrought iron and silver filigree, with silver runes along the blade’s edge.

When you inflict damage with a moonblade, any illusion or shape-changing magics that alter the target's form or appearance are immediately dispelled. If a magic-user is actively maintaining an effect, they may save vs. Curse to attempt to prevent one such effect from being dispelled by the moonblade.

Shapeshifting magic that is sustained by a wild shape talisman will only be dispelled for 1d6 combat rounds, after which the wild shape reasserts itself.

### Relic Weapon

*Legendary (Divine)*  

*cannot be forged*

Each relic weapon is unique, being the weapon used in life by a martyred saint.

When wielded by the righteous, a relic weapon erupts with a ghostly white nimbus of flame — attacks with it become easier and inflict an additional +3d6 radiant or aetheric damage (attacker’s choice). This damage is maximized against unholy creatures. Each relic weapon manifests the will of the saint who wielded it in life; actions which go against that saint’s personal convictions will withdraw the weapon’s bonuses.

### Sparkwhip

*Any Rarity (Arcane or Primal)*  

*Ingredients: Air Pearl, feysilver, copper*

A sparkwhip is a silvery chain whip enchanted with elemental Air energy. It adds 1d6 worth of lightning damage per rarity level, and attacks against targets wearing metal armor are easier. Any damage die that rolls a ‘6’ – whether a weapon damage die or a lightning damage die – entangles and restrains the target, allowing the attacker to automatically inflict the weapon’s lightning damage at the end of each combat round until the target saves vs. Hold to break free of the restraint.

### Sunlance

*Rare (Arcane or Primal)*  

*Ingredients: Flame Pearl, orichalc, iron, ruby*

A sunlance is a polearm enchanted with Elemental Flame energy, which adds two bonus dice of radiant damage whenever a melee attack with it hits. As an action, the wielder can aim the sunlance and make a ranged attack against any target within line of sight, with a base range of 25 paces. The sunlance fires a beam of searing golden light that inflicts 4d6 radiant damage on a hit, and blinds and stuns the target on a critical hit. A sunlance holds up to three charges of aetheric energy, and requires an hour to recharge each charge.

### Thunderhammer

*Any Rarity (Arcane or Primal)*  

*Ingredients: Thunder Pearl, orichalc, iron*

A thunderhammer is a bludgeon enhanced with elemental thunder energy. It adds 1d6 worth of thunder damage per rarity level whenever a melee attack with it hits, and its fire damage ignores armor.

If any damage die rolls a ‘6’, the target is stunned, and must also save vs. Hold or be knocked prone and thrown backwards a number of paces equal to the wielder’s Strength + twice the weapon’s rarity level.

### Venom Scythe

*Any Rarity (Arcane or Primal)*  

*Ingredients: Wood Pearl, orichalc, iron*

A venom scythe is a glaive enchanted with elemental Wood energy. It adds 1d6 additional poison damage per rarity level whenever a melee attack with it deals weapon damage, then forces a save vs. Doom or the target suffers ongoing poison damage.

### Windbow

*Any Rarity (Arcane or Primal)*  

*Ingredients: Wood Pearl, Air Pearl, feysilver, giant*

*spider silk, exotic wood*

A windbow is a shortbow, longbow or greatbow enchanted with elemental Air and Wood energy. It grants its rarity level as a bonus to to-hit and damage, and adds 10 paces per rarity level to its base range.
