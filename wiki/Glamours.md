## Stone Glamours

### Iron Ward

*First Circle*  

**Fast -** You make all attacks that target you with steel or iron weapons harder to hit you for as long as you maintain focus. If an attack is from a projectile weapon smaller than a dagger, such as a steel arrowhead or a cast iron bullet, you also halve its damage, and the projectile cannot lodge into your body or clothes.

### Tremorsense

*Second Circle*  

You touch your ear to solid stone, then strike it with a hard object, revealing the layout of any underground area within 5 paces of yourself (including vertically).

### Meld With Earth

*Third Circle*  

You step into a natural stone or earthen object or surface large enough to fully contain your body, melding yourself and all dedicated equipment with the earth for as long as you maintain focus. Nothing of your presence remains visible or otherwise detectable by non-magical senses. When this glamour ends, you fall prone in the closest unoccupied space. You cannot see or move while submerged, and any Alertness tests you make to hear nearby sounds are harder. Minor physical damage to the earth doesn’t harm you, but its destruction or a change in its shape or substance (to the extent that it is no longer earth or you no longer fit within it) ends the glamour and deals 2d6 blunt damage to you.

### Earthquake

*Fourth Circle*  

You cause a short but intense earthquake in the ground in a 5-pace radius. The area becomes difficult terrain until cleared. While you maintain focus, you may use your action each round to make an area attack, forcing each creature other than you in that area to save vs. Hold or suffer 3d6 blunt damage and be knocked prone.

This spell can also be used to directly control up to a roughly 3-pace radius of earth or stone centered on yourself for as long as you maintain focus, fusing it into rock or breaking it into sand, or shaping it as you desire. Stone structures will hold their shape if sturdy enough to do so.

## Water Glamours

### Cleansing Waters

*First Circle*  

You touch one living creature, drenching it with cooling water and drawing out any negative influences. Any ongoing damage is instantly ended, and you grant the creature an immediate saving throw vs. a single effect that a save could end.

### Purify Food and Drink

*Second Circle*  

You may flush up to 2d6 x 10 lbs of nonmagical food or drink with vital energy as you touch it, cleansing it of any poisons or diseases.

When performed as a ritual, you may instead infuse a smaller amount of food or drink harvested fresh from the local environment with a lingering healing energy. Each bite of food or swallow of drink so enchanted restores 1 hp, and provides enough nourishment to sustain a medium creature for one day. This second ritual form of the glamour is often called the goodberry ritual. When you perform the goodberry ritual, you produce 2d6 portions. Each portion will last until the next sunrise or sunset before reverting to a normal bite of food or drink.

### Create Water

*Third Circle*  

You create 1d6 gallons of potable water; enough to last a single medium creature that many weeks.

### Tidal Wave

*Fourth Circle*  

You cast this glamour while adjacent to a large body of water to summon a vast tidal wave. The wave propagates in a 10-pace cone from you, dealing 6d6 blunt damage to each creature and knocking it prone unless it saves vs. Hold. (Creatures who successfully save only suffer half damage.)

This spell can also be used to directly control up to a roughly 5-pace radius area of water or ice centered on yourself for as long as you maintain focus, either freezing or melting it, or shaping and flowing it as you desire to cover up to nine square paces worth of ground. Ice structures will hold their shape if sturdy enough to do so.

## Air Glamours

### Fog Cloud

*First Circle*  

You create a 10-pace-radius sphere of fog that billows out from a space within 5 paces, spreading around corners and obscuring everything, for 2d6 exploration turns.

### Fresh Air

*Second Circle*  

You touch one living creature. While you maintain focus, that creature can hold its breath indefinitely.

### Gust of Wind

*Third Circle*  

A blast of hurricane-force wind rushes out from you in a 5-pace cone that inflicts 2d6 thunder damage. Each creature within the area must save vs. Hold or be knocked prone and thrown backwards 1d6+1 paces, suffering another 2d6 blunt damage on landing.

Unsecured objects are automatically pushed away from you, but heavier objects will only be pushed back half this distance; exceptionally dense objects will only be pushed back one pace.

### Call Storm

*Fourth Circle*  

You begin this glamour as your action while outdoors in a heavy storm. For as long as you maintain focus, you may use your action each round to influence the storm for 25 paces around you. If cast as a ritual, you may conjure a storm from a clear sky over the course of an hour, rolling 2d6 to determine the number of hours that the storm will last. You may also use this ritual to calm a storm in progress, dissipating it into clear skies. Two druids performing this ritual at crosspurposes within a mile of each other each make a Survival test to determine which ritual succeeds, with no net effect happening if both succeed or both fail.

Controlling a Storm

Winds can be used to push affected creatures; your action may

be spent to target any creatures within a circle 10 paces wide

or a cone 40 paces deep, knocking them prone and moving them

1d6 paces in a direction of your choice.

Lightning called from the clouds inflicts 5d6 damage in a two-

pace blast; creatures in metal armor have harder saves.

Hail and sleet deal 1d6 cold damage to each creature within a

25-pace area (save vs. Hold for half damage); well-insulated

waterproof clothing makes this saving throw easier.

## Flame Glamours

### Protection from Heat and Cold

*First Circle*  

You may touch a creature, granting it protection from temperature extremes while you maintain focus. All fire and cold damage is halved, and any other environmental effects related to temperature that would make actions harder are ignored.

### Heat Metal

*Second Circle*  

You choose one manufactured metal object – such as a metal weapon or a suit of heavy or medium metal armor – that you can see within 10 paces, causing it to glow red-hot. Any creature in physical contact with that object immediately takes 2d6 fire damage.

Any creature that takes this damage must save or drop the object if it can. If it doesn’t or can’t drop the object, all its rolls are harder. While you focus, you can use your action each round to cause this damage again.

### Sunlight

*Third Circle*  

You raise your arms above your head and conjure an orb of bright, golden sunlight.

While you maintain focus, it casts bright light out to 10 paces and dim light out to 20 paces. As your action on subsequent turns, you may focus it into a searing ray of light that deals 2d6 radiant damage in a line 20 paces long.

### Wall of Flame

*Fourth Circle*  

You cast this glamour while adjacent to a large bonfire (covering at least one five foot square) to rapidly control its growth and spread, blazing it into up to ten contiguous square paces each up to ten feet high. While you focus, you can reshape the wall as you see fit, so long as it fits within ten continuous paces and at least one pace is adjacent to yourself. The wall inflicts 3d6 fire damage to any creatures that are engulfed or who attempt to pass through the wall; a creature may save vs. Blast as it is engulfed.

## Wood Glamours

### Lifesense

*First Circle*  

While you maintain focus, you can see all living beings in your line of sight, whether invisible, concealed, or camouflaged. This vision is unaffected by light or darkness, fog, or foliage, but cannot penetrate through solid earth, stone, or unliving wood thicker than one foot, metal thicker than one inch, or cold iron or living wood of any thickness.

If you touch an ingestible substance while focusing on this glamour, you immediately know whether  is safe to eat or drink. If you touch a living creature, you learn its species, size, sex, state of health, and what physical conditions are affecting it - including whether it suffers from any poisons or diseases, whether they are treatable, and what treatments are likely to be effective.

### Protection from Poison

*Second Circle*  

You may touch a creature, granting it protection from toxins and diseases while you maintain focus. The protected creature is immune to all poison damage or effects, and automatically succeeds at any saving throw vs. any effect caused by a poison or disease.

### Restore Health

*Third Circle*  

You touch a creature, either allowing it to roll one hit die and heal its level + that many hit points, or instantly removing one poison or disease affecting it.

### Wild Growth

*Fourth Circle*  

This glamour channels vitality into plants within a specific area. Choose a point within 20 paces; all normal plants in a 10-pace radius centered on that point become thick, overgrown, and bristling with long, sharp thorns. You can exclude one or more patches of any size within the area from being affected. You can instead choose a line 2 paces wide and 25 paces long, creating a wall of brambles 5 feet high.

A creature must spend its action and save vs. Hold to attempt to move forward one pace through the area; even if they succeed, the thorns cause 1d6 piercing damage per pace moved. Creatures may instead attempt to hack it down by attacking it with suitable bladed weapons; each 5-foot patch of the area has 1D6 structural hit points, and halves all damage inflicted on it that isn’t sundering or fire damage. While you focus and remain within the area, trees, roots, vines, and grasses within 5 paces of you become animated, with the following effects:

**Grasses and Undergrowth -** Any area of ground in the cube that is covered by grass or undergrowth is difficult terrain.

**Trees -** At the end of each round of combat, each of your enemies within 10 feet of any tree in the area must save vs. blast or take four dice of damage from whipping branches.

**Roots and Vines -** At the end of each round of combat, one creature of your choice that is in the affected area must save vs. Hold or be knocked prone and restrained until the glamour ends. A restrained creature can use its action to attempt to break free.

**Nature's Bounty -** You may instead touch a living edible plant and channel vitality into it, causing it to cycle through its growing season over several seconds and immediately yield a rich harvest sufficient to provide 1d6+1 nourishing meals.

When cast as a ritual, you may alternatively channel vitality within a specific area with long-term benefits. All plants in a half-mile radius centered on you become enriched for one year, doubling their harvest and making all Survival checks to cultivate the land easier.

## Beast Glamours

Each of these glamours directly interacts with animals or fey beasts in some way. A beast glamour is prepared as a wood talisman unless the target is a fey beast with any elemental powers, in which case it must be prepared with the appropriate element.

### Call Beasts

*First Circle*  

You send out a subtle psychic summons, intermingled with natural sounds and scents that will attract the attention of any nearby animals. Make a Survival check; if you succeed, a natural or fey beast (or possibly a herd of them) arrives within a number of exploration turns based on their distance and previous activities. You may specify a particular kind of natural animal or fey beast as you cast this glamour, or you may emit a general summons to all nearby creatures.

### Speak With Animals

*Second Circle*  

You make eye contact with a natural animal or fey beast, and establish an empathic bond between yourself and that creature. This allows the exchange of simple concepts, emotions, and requests through wordless communication for as long as you focus.

An intelligent creature can end the connection at-will, and must be bargained with to obey any requests; creatures with Intelligence of -4 or less will obey any non-harmful action that they can understand unless they succeed at a save vs. Trick.

When cast as a ritual, you may speak a short message to a tiny animal within 5 paces that you can see, while thinking of a location that you have visited and a description of a person that you expect to be in that location.

The glamour weaves its way into the animal, which remains enchanted for 2d6 days. The animal then immediately swims, scurries, or flies off at its speed towards its destination. Upon arrival, the animal magically speaks the message you gave it to the first person it sees matching the description you gave, roughly duplicating the sound and tone of your voice. If the glamour runs out before the animal can find a suitable recipient, the message is lost, and the animal magically finds its way home as the enchantment unweaves.

### Beast Bond

*Third Circle*  

You make eye contact with a natural animal or fey beast within 5 paces. You beguile that creature until the next sunrise or sunset unless it saves vs. Trick. Its saving throw is easy if it has more hit dice than your level, or hard if your level is more than twice its hit dice. The effect ends immediately if you or your allies harm the creature in any way.

If you cast this glamour on a creature as a ritual at every sunrise and sunset for a lunar month, the creature becomes trained and loyal to you if you continue to treat it well.

### Polymorph

*Fourth Circle*  

You may use a prepared Wild Shape talisman to forcibly transform a living creature into the talisman's stored shape. This transformation does not protect the target's intelligence. The target must save vs. Curse to resist being transformed, and remains transformed until they touch the talisman that transformed them. You may also coax one of your elemental wisp companions to use this glamour on itself, to grow in size and manifest before you in the shape of any elemental, awakened plant, fey beast, or fey or elemental monster appropriate to the wisp’s elemental nature, with all of that creature’s abilities and hit points. You or the chosen wisp must have interacted with a creature of the chosen species before, and the referee is the final arbiter of whether this glamour can emulate the traits of any particular creature. The creature is under the referee’s control, but is loyal to you and will obey your commands. If its hit points drop to zero, it shrinks back to Tiny size and finds its way back into your pouch. Otherwise, this glamour ends at the next sunrise or sunset.

You may perform this glamour as a ritual to permanently merge a wisp of the appropriate element into a tree or a natural animal with 5 or fewer hit dice and an Intelligence of -4, granting the recipient of this ritual true intelligence. An awakened tree becomes a Treant, while an awakened animal gains an Intelligence based on the results of a d6 roll: 13: -3, 4-5: -2, 6: -1. It knows one language that you speak, and awakens with a friendly disposition towards you; it will continue to serve you or not depending upon how it is treated by you and your allies.

## Dream Glamours

### Sleep

*First Circle*  

You make eye contact with another living creature or creatures within 5 paces of you whose total levels are less than twice your own. Each affected creature is stunned for one combat round, then must save vs Curse or fall unconscious for 1d6 exploration turns. A creature higher than your own level rolls an easy saving throw; a creature less than half your own level rolls a hard saving throw.

Once asleep, an affected creature cannot wake on its own, and any attempt to rouse the creature is hard.

### Phantasm

*Second Circle*  

You create the image of an object, a creature, or some other phenomenon that is no larger than a 20-foot cube. It appears at a spot that you can see within 15 paces, and seems completely real, including any sounds, smells, or other appropriate sensations. A creature that closely examines it can save vs. Trick to notice that it is an illusion. As long as you focus, you can cause it to move to any other spot within range, while altering it so that its movements, sounds, and so on appear natural (such as making a phantasmal creature appear to walk, or even carry on a conversation).

### Suggestion

*Third Circle*  

You make eye contact with a target within 5 paces and suggest a belief or course of activity (no more than a sentence or two). Unless they save vs. Trick, they must follow or believe your suggestion for as long as you focus.

Whenever they would be presented with evidence that contradicts your suggestion, they may make another saving throw to attempt to snap out of it.

This spell has no effect if the target cannot hear or understand you, and the target’s saving throw is easy if your command is obviously and immediately harmful to it.

### Confusion

*Fourth Circle*  

This spell assaults and twists creatures’ minds, spawning delusions and provoking uncontrolled actions. Each creature within 3 paces that can hear you must save vs. Trick or be confused. Each creature confused by this spell rolls a d6 at the start of each round of combat to determine its behavior:

1 - The creature uses its action to make a melee attack

against a randomly determined creature within its reach, then

becomes stunned. If there is no creature within its reach, the

creature does nothing this round.

2, 3 - The creature uses all its movement to move in a

random direction. It then becomes stunned.

4, 5 - The creature is for stunned this round.

6 - The creature can act and move normally this round. At the

end of the round, it can save vs. Trick to attempt to snap out

of it; if it succeeds, the effect ends for this creature.

## Song Glamours

### Fascinate

*First Circle*  

You use your action to begin moving and speaking hypnotically. Each creature within 20 paces that can see and hear you must save vs. Trick or become oblivious to everything but you. You may weave this effect into a musical performance if you wish.

If you move at a normal pace, each enthralled creature will follow you if physically able. While you focus and continue speaking or performing, each affected creature must succeed at a hard Alertness test to notice any hazard or creature other than you.

### Beguile

*Second Circle*  

You attempt to beguile an intelligent mortal creature within 5 paces that makes eye contact with you or can hear your voice. If it saves vs. Trick, it knows that you attempted to manipulate its mind, and its attitude likely degrades by one step. If it fails, its attitude towards you becomes Friendly for as long as you focus. The effect immediately ends if you do anything harmful to the beguiled creature; if they suffer injury from any other source, they also immediately make a saving throw to attempt to snap out of it.

### Inspiration

*Third Circle*  

You begin singing a song or chant of bravery and valor. While you focus, you radiate an aura of inspiration and courage. Once per round, you may choose to allow an ally within 5 paces that can see or hear you to reroll an attack, save, or check; their new roll is easier.

### Irresistible Dance

*Fourth Circle*  

You begin singing a song or chanting, calling those around you to join you in compelled celebration. While you focus, each creature within 5 paces that can hear you must save vs. Curse at the end of each round to break free of the effect, or it is helpless and forced to perform a comic dance as its action, while moving in whichever direction you choose.

Glamours and Bardsongs

Bards can perform Dream and Song glamours as 'bardsongs',

which are a unique form of magic that does not require the

use of materia. A bard performing a bardsong taps into the

deepest, most primordial rules of faerie, beyond even the

eldest and wisest druid's understanding.

While a druid performs their glamours by directly shaping the

flow of elemental energy as it weaves its way between faerie

and the material world, a bard performs a bardsong through

pure skill and passion, inviting the semi-sentient energies of

faerie to take notice and reward the bard's performance with

the effects of the glamour.

In practice, this means that a bard performing a bardsong

takes longer to achieve a magical effect than a druid casting

a glamour directly, and the druid's glamour is guaranteed to

work (barring interference from opposing magic or cold iron),

while the bardsong has only a chance of success based on the

skill of the bard and the circle of the glamour attempted.

## Wyrd Glamours

### Fey Luck

*First Circle*  

**Fast -** You force another creature within 5 paces to reroll an attack throw, saving throw, or task check at an easier or harder difficulty.

### Augury

*Second Circle*  

By casting lots using talisman-enchanted runestones, you receive an omen about the likely results of a specific course of action that you plan to take within the next 30 minutes. The answer doesn’t take into account any possible supernatural circumstances that might change the outcome. The referee chooses from the following possible omens:

- Weal, for good results

- Woe, for bad results

- Weal and woe, for both good and bad

- Nothing, if results aren’t good or bad

### Primal Geas

*Third Circle*  

You force a creature that can see and hear you within 20 paces to save vs. Curse. If they fail, you place a powerful curse on them, forcing them to carry out some service or refrain from some action or course of activity while you maintain focus. You may use this glamour to sanctify an oath or boast made in your presence, binding the oath-swearer to their word. If you do, their save becomes harder.

If cast as a ritual, your curse may be worded so that only a certain action or circumstance may break it. If the target fails to save, and the referee determines that the conditions are possible to fulfill, the curse becomes utterly permanent until those conditions are met - not even a remove curse miracle can break it. You cannot undo such a curse once it is done.

### Reincarnate

*Fourth Circle*  

You invoke the spirits of nature to create a new body for a fallen comrade. By interring a creature’s remains in the fresh earth of a faerie grove and planting a magically-imbued seed, you consume the accumulated fey energies of the sacred grove to cause a Large flower-bud to grow out of their remains overnight.

This ritual fails if the interred creature has been dead for longer than one lunar month.

Otherwise, if the interred creature’s soul is able to return to the material world, the bud blooms at dawn — revealing the slumbering form of a newly created body that contains that creature’s memories and personality.

The new form is crafted from the chaotic energies of Faerie, and so it will not necessarily be of the same sex or species as the creature’s original body. Roll a d20 and consult the following chart to determine the creature’s new species. The referee is encouraged to modify this list based on the location the ritual is performed, the phase of the moon, or any other fickle whims of Faerie. Any curses, diseases, or lasting injuries that the creature’s previous body suffered from are not transferred to the new body, but any particularly memorable scars or afflictions might manifest as a birthmark or other pattern on their new form. The reincarnated creature retains all of their original memories and personality, including talents and class levels, but re-rolls their abilities.

Their new body will be 1d6 years old when the flower opens, and will continue to grow at an accelerated rate until it reaches young physical maturity, although a particularly strong-willed creature might manage to will their body to stop aging at a younger or older point while the magic is still fresh. They will have reached a stable age by sundown, aging normally from that point forward.

## Mist Glamours

### Faerie Grove

*First Circle*  

By binding a Wood materia, a Dream materia, and an Aether materia together and burying them in the earth while whispering a softly susurrating song,  you pull Faerie and the Material closer together within a circular area up to 30 paces in radius. Some kind of border marker, usually a ring of mushrooms or a wild thorny hedge, will spring out of the ground around the borders as you complete the glamour. Within the grove, all primal magics are easier and all other forms of magic are harder until the next sunrise or sunset. If you perform this ritual in the same place each sunrise and sunset for one lunar month, and leave at least one wisp of each elemental aspect within the area to tend to the grove, all effects and defenses become permanent until dispelled. Other glamours may be woven into a permanent grove’s defenses and features as well: awakened animals or treants may be bound to its protection, or elemental defenses such as mirage, nature's wrath, or call storm may be woven into the grove as rituals, to be called on by any druid that is recognized by the grove as part of its circle.

Druids who maintain a permanent Faerie Grove usually continue to perform the necessary rituals at each sunrise and sunset, weaving their magic deeper and deeper into the bones of the earth. A permanent grove that has been maintained for less than a decade is easy to dispel, while a permanent grove that has been maintained for a century or more is hard to dispel.

### Misty Step

*Second Circle*  

On your action instead of moving, you conjure a silvery mist from Faerie, which quickly dissipates as you teleport up to 5 paces away into an unoccupied space that you can see.

If cast as a ritual at sunrise or sunset within a faerie grove, you open a portal directly between Faerie and the Material, which remains open until the next sunrise or sunset.

### Leyline Travel

*Third Circle*  

As an action, you touch a surface or archway made of living plant matter - such as a tree or vine-covered wall - and open a portal through Faerie. An identical portal appears within a faerie grove that you have set foot in since the last sunrise or sunset. You may maintain focus to keep the portals open, allowing others to follow you.

Make a Survival test with an easy difficulty for a portal within one league, or at hard difficulty for a portal more than eight leagues away. On a failure, you step out somewhere in between, determined randomly.

### Mirage

*Fourth Circle*  

You cast this ritual to make the terrain around you look, sound, and smell like some other sort of natural terrain. The glamour’s effect flows out from you in a sphere that grows one pace per round in radius to a maximum of 100 paces in every direction, transforming your surroundings as the wave of magic passes over them. Open fields can be made to resemble difficult or impassable terrain. A pond can be made to seem like a grassy meadow, a precipice like a gentle slope, or a rock-strewn gully like a wide and smooth valley. Manufactured structures, equipment, and creatures within the area aren’t changed in appearance.

The tactile characteristics of the terrain are unchanged, so creatures entering the area are likely to see through the illusion. If the difference isn’t obvious by touch, a creature carefully examining the illusion can attempt a save to disbelieve it.
