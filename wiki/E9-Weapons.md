A weapon's **size** determines its speed, damage, how many hands it takes to wield it, and whether it can be held in the off hand. Some weapons have additional properties, such as piercing, entangling, blunt, or sundering, described below.

**Light** weapons always deal 1d6 damage and strike before non-light weapons; **heavy** weapons always deal 2d6+Str damage and strike after non-heavy weapons. Weapons that are neither light nor heavy (**medium**) deal 1d6+Str damage when wielded one-handed, or 2d6 damage when wielded two-handed. A creature wielding a weapon in each hand makes one attack throw at the speed of the slower weapon, then adds both weapons' damage together if the attack hits.

## Weapon Sizes

### Melee

| Size | Damage | Attack speed | Wielded |
|---|---|---|---|
| Light | 1d6 | Fast | Off- or one-handed |
| Medium | 1d6+Str | Normal | One-handed\* |
| Medium | 2d6 | Normal | Two-handed |
| Heavy | 2d6+Str | Slow | Two-handed\* |

### Ranged

| Size | Damage | Attack speed | Wielded |
|---|---|---|---|
| Light | 1d6 | Fast | Off- or one-handed |
| Medium | 1d6 | Normal | Two-handed |
| Heavy | 2d6 | Slow | Two-handed\* |

\* Due to their small size, [dwarves](E9-Origins#dwarf) and [gnomes](E9-Origins#gnome) can't wield heavy weapons, and must wield medium weapons two-handed.

## Weapon Properties

### Blunt

On any 6 rolled for damage, the target staggers: it saves vs. Hold or is knocked down or pushed back (attacker's choice). Doom saves to resist a critical hit from a blunt weapon are easier. See [Weapon Damage](E9-Damage#weapon-damage).

### Entangling

On any 6 rolled for damage, the target saves vs. Hold or is knocked down or disarmed (attacker's choice). An entangling weapon can make a [grab](E9-Combat#grab-or-shove). Doom saves to resist a critical hit from an entangling weapon are easier. See [Weapon Damage](E9-Damage#weapon-damage).

### Piercing

Ignores light or medium armor when inflicting damage. A braced piercing polearm ignores all armor (see [Brace](E9-Combat#brace)).

### Sundering

Each 6 rolled for damage reduces the target's armor by 1 until repaired; if the target has a shield, any 6 destroys the shield instead. It is easier to hit you with an interrupt attack while you are attacking with a sundering weapon. See [Weapon Damage](E9-Damage#weapon-damage).

### Polearm

Can attack targets 2 paces away and can interrupt approaching enemies. Oversized: +1 weight for [encumbrance](E9-Encumbrance).

### Reach

Can attack targets up to 3 paces away.

### Thrown

Can be thrown, with the three listed ranges (short / medium / long, in paces).

### Range

Ranged weapons list three ranges (short / medium / long, in paces).

## Reach and Range

Weapons with the polearm property can attack targets 2 paces away and can interrupt approaching enemies, while weapons with the reach property can attack targets up to 3 paces away. Ranged or thrown weapons have three listed ranges; attacks are **easy** within short range, and **hard** at long range or at point-blank range.
