## Talents

There are 12 talents, each linked to a different ability. Each talent determines the threshold for various d6 task checks, which are made by rolling a d6 and comparing it to that talent’s threshold. Talents begin at a threshold of 5+ if your linked ability is +0; if your linked ability is better or worse, that many talents linked to that ability begin at 4+ or 6+ instead. You may choose which talents to assign your 6+, 5+, or 4+ thresholds to from each ability.

Your Dexterity talents are Agility, Finesse, and Stealth. **Agility** is your full-body athleticism, and is used for climbing and other athletic feats. **Finesse** is your hand-eye coordination, and is used for detailed work like picking a lock or assembling a clockwork contraption.

**Stealth** is your ability to move your body within an environment, to avoid making noise or being seen.

Your Intelligence talents are Lore, Medicine, and Science. **Lore** is your linguistic ability, "book learning" and capacity to remember trivia, while **Medicine** is your understanding of living bodies and how to maintain or restore their health, and **Science** is your grasp of physical and magical phenomena and the rules that govern them.

Your Wisdom talents are Alertness, Intuition, and Survival. Your **Alertness** measures your ability to pick up details and clues from your environment, determine which ones are important, and know how to respond to them.

**Intuition** is your "common sense" and how well you can listen to your gut, both in social situations and in more unusual circumstances. Survival is your ability to navigate the wilds and survive within them, including finding or creating shelter, food, water, and other necessities..

Your Charisma talents are Bearing, Charm, and Wit. **Bearing** is your ability to project authority, command respect, and maintain your composure in tense situations. **Charm** is your ability to project trust, warmth, or anything else that could influence another creature's long-term attitude towards you.

Finally, **Wit** is your ability to be persuasive, whether you are trying to change another creature's beliefs or just convince them to perform some task or action.

## Ability Checks

Sometimes, the result of an action will be determined purely by your character's ability, regardless of any training or experience they might have. An Ability check rolls a d6 and adds the appropriate ability. (In a sense, Hit Dice are a kind of ability check.) The referee determines the threshold for success based on the circumstances.

## Easier and Harder Rolls

Any attack throw, saving throw, task check, or ability check that your character might roll can be made easier or harder by various circumstances. When circumstances combine to make a roll easy, you roll two dice and use the better roll. When circumstances combine to make a roll hard, you roll two dice and must use the worse roll. If a roll is neither hard nor easy, you roll a single die.

If multiple circumstances make a situation easier while other circumstances make the same situation harder, each circumstance making the situation easier cancels out one circumstance making the situation harder, unless the referee decides that a particular circumstance overwhelms all the others.

If there are multiple circumstances all making a situation easier or harder, the referee might declare that it is now impossible to succeed or fail at that task, and not call for a roll at all.

| Difficulty | Roll |
|---|---|
| Automatic | No roll necessary |
| Easy | roll 2 dice (choose better) |
| Normal | roll 1 die |
| Hard | roll 2 dice (choose worse) |

Impossible No roll possible

### Multiple Attempts

Unless the referee decides otherwise, each subsequent attempt of the same action is one difficulty step harder than the last, as you run out of things to try.

### Rewarding Ingenuity

A large part of a fantasy adventure game is coming up with clever solutions to problems. If the party's approach to a particular task is particularly clever or effective, the referee should lower the difficulty of that task check accordingly.
