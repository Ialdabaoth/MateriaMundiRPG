# Wizard

### ()

*Human Magic-User*  

### Class Features

#### Prime Ability:

*Intelligence*  

#### Hit Die:

*d6+Con*  

#### Recommended Starting Equipment

Spellbook, dagger, quarterstaff, robes, potion belt, dungeoneer’s kit

#### Martial Training

Wizards are not familiar with any non-simple weapons or armor. Additionally, wearing any armor at all interferes with their ability to prepare and cast arcane magic.

#### Talent Training

At levels 2 and 6, you gain training in **Lore**; at levels 4 and 8, you gain training in **Science**.

## Arcane Spellcasting

You begin play with a spellbook, which holds a number of different spells of various circles determined by the referee. As you find other spellbooks or spell scrolls in the dungeon, you may copy them into your own spellbook.

Each sunrise or sunset, you can prepare a number of arcane spells from your spellbook based on your level, which you can cast during the day. Once a spell is cast, it becomes un-prepared. You can prepare spells of a lower circle than indicated.

#### Preparing During Exploration

You can prepare spells during exploration, if you have an empty preparation of a suitable circle. This takes a number of Science checks equal to the spell’s circle; each roll takes one turn spent studying a spellbook with the chosen spell and applying its arcana.

#### Cantrips

Each spell also gives you a cantrip, which is a smaller version of the spell that can be cast atwill without un-preparing the spell.

#### Arcane Rituals

You can also cast arcane spells from your spellbook as rituals; each ritual takes one turn per circle. You may perform any spell up to a circle of half your level (round up) as a ritual. Each round that you perform a ritual requires a Science check, or the ritual fails. If each roll succeeds, a ritual's effect happens on the next turn after it is completed.

#### Arcane Magic and Armor

If you wear light armor, you can maintain one fewer preparation of each circle that you can prepare. If you wear medium or heavy armor, you cannot cast prepared spells at all.
