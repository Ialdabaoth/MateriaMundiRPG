## Belts

A belt is any magic item worn around the torso. Many enchanted satchels and backpacks are designed to be worn as belts.

### Bag of Holding

*Uncommon+ (Arcane)*  

*Ingredients: Aether Pearl, feysilver, giant spider*

*silk*

A bag of holding appears to be a fine leather pouch from the outside, roughly twelve inches wide, three inches thick and six inches deep. When opened, however, runes of metallic thread are stitched around the rim of the pocket, and the pouch itself appears bottomless.

An uncommon bag of holding can store up to 15 cubic feet of objects. When you attempt to place an object in the bag, as long as its smallest dimension is less than 5 feet wide, the bag’s opening expands to accommodate the item, returning to its normal size once the object is fully inside. A rare bag of holding can store up to 125 cubic feet of objects, while some Legendary bags of holding can store 1,000 cubic feet or more.

As your action, you may reach into the bag and mentally choose an item. If that object is in the bag, it summons to your hand and can be pulled out, feeling nearly weightless until the last inch of it clears the lip of the bag. Bags of holding are notoriously vulnerable to Aetheric magic. If a keen blade or other sharp Aetheric item is placed into the bag unsheathed, or if any Aetheric magic item that produces teleportation, size-changing, or other effects is placed into the bag, the referee will make a hidden saving throw to determine if the bag’s aetheric pocket is pierced. The results of a torn bag can range from all the items spilling out at once around the wearer, to a rip in the veil that sucks the wearer - and anyone else within several paces - into the deep aether. For the same reason, placing a portable hole into a bag of holding (even when closed), or wearing a bag of holding while passing through a portable hole, is an immensely stupid idea.

### Belt of Feral Might

*Any Rarity (Primal)*  

*Ingredients: Wood Pearl, exotic leather*

A belt of feral might adds its rarity bonus to the wearer’s Strength and Constitution while worn, adjusting their melee damage, hit points, attack and saving throws, and raw ability checks. If another magical effect also increases the wearer’s abilities, only the highest effect takes hold.

### Quiver of Elemental Bolts

*Any Rarity (Arcane or Primal)*  

| | |
|---|---|
| *Ingredients:* | *Dragon Pearl, feysilver, orichalc,* |

*exotic leather*

A quiver of elemental bolts looks like a finely crafted arrow or crossbow quiver from the outside, holding up to a dozen arrows or two dozen crossbow bolts. Inside the pocket, inlaid metal-woven threads are stitched in arcane patterns, slowly gathering and focusing elemental energies into any arrows or crossbow bolts placed inside.

When you place a cluster of arrows or bolts into the quiver, on the next sunrise or sunset the inlaid arcana will infuse those arrows with sufficient arcane energy from the dragon pearl to grant each of them +1d6 elemental damage per rarity level.

You may also charge one arrow or bolt as you draw it, infusing it with a much larger amount of elemental energy. That missile will detonate when fired at a range you specify, unleashing a blast area attack with a radius of 2 paces. Any creature caught within the blast must save or suffer 3d6 elemental damage. If you make an attack throw against a creature with a charged arrow, it suffers 6d6 of elemental damage and a critical hit effect on a hit, and the area of effect is halved to 1 pace. If you miss, the arrow still detonates in the target’s space, unleashing the full 2-pace radius 3d6 blast.

Once you use this charge, a common quiver requires one sunrise or sunset to recharge, while an uncommon quiver requires only an hour to recharge, and a rare quiver requires ten minutes.
