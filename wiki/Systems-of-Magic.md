## Systems of Magic

There are four systems of magic presented in this book, each of which belongs to a different character class. Each system draws power from the aether in its own unique way, shaping it into effects that seem to twist the laws of physics as mortals understand them.

All magic pulls energy from the aether, which is a "parallel world" that both surrounds and permeates our own, and also extends beyond our own out to infinity. The portions of the aether nearest to our own reality manifest as a pair of "mirror worlds", most often called

*faerie* and *shadow*. Faerie is a world of chaotic

elemental energies and burgeoning life-force, while shadow is a world of dark stability dotted both with shining beacons of hope and purpose, and pools of despair and decay.

Different magic systems interact with the aether in different ways to produce their effects. Primal druidcraft pulls its energy exclusively through faerie to shape the elemental and living forces of our world according to its eldritch whims. Divine miracles, on the other hand, pull power exclusively through shadow, calling on faith or the favor of ancestors to bend fate towards desired outcomes. Arcane power, which includes both wizardcraft and dwarven dweomersmithing, pulls from either realm; the complexity of arcane effects necessitates complex patterns of runes, sigils, and aetheric flow lines to "program" channeled energies into a precise desired effect.
