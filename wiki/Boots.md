## Boots

Boots are any magic item designed to be worn around the lower legs or feet. Creatures with unusual anatomy may require that boot-like magic items be custom made for their body shape.

### Boots of Stability

*Common (Arcane)*  

*Ingredients: Earth Pearl, orichalc, roper leather*

Boots of stability appear to be a pair of fine leather boots from the outside. The inside of the boots are lined with runes and sigils in metallic thread. While wearing boots of stability, all saving throws vs. Hold to avoid being knocked down are easy, and difficult terrain does not cost you extra movement or risk tripping you. You may react when you would be moved or knocked prone to anchor yourself firmly to whatever surface you are standing on; until you release yourself as a combat action, you cannot be knocked prone or moved from that spot. Once used, this anchoring ability requires an hour to recharge.

### Boots of Swiftness

*Uncommon (Arcane or Primal)*  

*Ingredients: Air Pearl, feysilverl, bloodwing leather*

Boots of Swiftness appear to be a pair of fine leather boots from the outside. The inside of the boots are lined with runes and sigils in metallic thread. While wearing Boots of Swiftness, you can move each round as if you had performed the dash action.

### Boots of Traceless Passage

*Common (Arcane or Primal)*  

*Ingredients: Dream Pearl, feysilver, exotic leather*

Boots of traceless passage appear to be a pair of fine leather boots from the outside. The inside of the boots are lined with runes and sigils in metallic thread. While wearing Boots of Traceless Passage, your footsteps make no sound, even if wearing heavy armor, and you leave no footprints or other marks of your passage.

### Grip Boots

*Rare (Arcane or Primal)*  

*Ingredients: giant gecko hide, orichalc, Earth Pearl*

Grip boots appear to be a pair of lizard-skin boots with ridged soles. Closer inspection will reveal small metallic runes woven within the sole ridges. While wearing grip boots, you can walk on any surface, even upside-down.

### Golem Prosthesis

*Uncommon+ (Arcane)*  

*Ingredients: ironwood, devil creeper, jade,*

*hippogriff leather, Wood Pearl*

A golem prosthesis is an artificial leg, designed to replace a maimed or amputated limb. A single replaced leg is an uncommon item, and operates nearly identically to the wearer’s original limb, while also granting a +1 to Armor when not wearing medium or heavy armor. A pair of matched legs count as a single rare boots item, grant +2 to Armor when not wearing medium or heavy armor, and may have another common or uncommon boots enchantment incorporated into their design, while a legendary pair of leg prostheses can incorporate a rare boots enchantment.

### Seven-League Boots

*Legendary (Arcane)*  

*Ingredients: Aether Pearl, orichalc, exotic leather*

Seven-league boots appear to be a pair of fine leather boots from the outside. The inside of the boots are lined with runes and sigils in metallic thread. Once per hour while wearing seven-league boots, you may roll a Science or Survival check to teleport yourself seven leagues along a current direction of travel. The difficulty is determined by the terrain of the area you are teleporting into; success lands you on a road or clearing, while failure lands you lost and in difficult terrain. A critical failure results in 3d6 aetheric damage, as you teleport into a space occupied by an object. Any damage die that rolls a ‘6’ results in a critical hit and maiming.
