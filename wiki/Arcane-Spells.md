## Cantrips

Each Wizard spell has a cantrip effect. Many spells share the same cantrip effect, so they are listed here and referred to by name in each spell’s description. Any cantrip that deals damage always deals the same type of damage as the prepared spell.

#### Detect Magic

While a spell with the Detect Magic cantrip is prepared, you sense the presence of any active magic within 3 paces of you. When you notice a magic effect, you may make a hard Science check to determine what kind of magic it is (arcane, divine, or primal), or to determine what schools of magic it belongs to if arcane.

#### Elemental Grasp

While a spell with the Elemental Grasp cantrip is prepared, you may make a melee attack throw with a bare hand that inflicts 1d6 damage of the spell's prepared element.

#### Flickering Glow

While a spell with the Flickering Glow cantrip is prepared, you may create a torch-sized orb of light in your hand. This orb sheds dim light in a 2-pace radius in a color of your choice.

#### Friends

While a spell with the Friends cantrip is prepared, any Charm or Wit test vs. a mortal within 5 paces who is not hostile towards you is easy for as long as you maintain focus and eye contact. When this effect ends, the target knows that you influenced its mind, and their attitude towards you degrades by 1d6 steps.

#### Jump

While a spell with the Jump cantrip is prepared, your jumping distance is doubled, and you halve any falling damage that you take. You may also touch another adjacent creature as it performs the jump action to double its jump distance, but its falling damage is not halved.

#### Mage Hand

While a spell with the Mage Hand cantrip is prepared, you may project a ghostly shadow of one of your own hands up to 5 paces away. The hand can manipulate items, open doors or containers, or perform appropriate tests and saving throws, but cannot attack, activate magic items, or carry any object weighing more than 10 lbs.

#### Magic Missile

While a spell with the Magic Missile cantrip is prepared, you may make a ranged attack throw with a 10 pace range that inflicts 1d6 damage of the same type as the prepared spell.

#### Resistance

**Fast -** While a spell with the Resistance cantrip is prepared, you halve all damage you receive from any source of the prepared element whenever you evade.

#### Shroud

While a spell with the Shroud cantrip is prepared, your Stealth checks to hide are easier.

#### Spell Ward

While a spell with the Spell Ward cantrip is prepared, your saving throws vs. spells, miracles, or other magical effects are easier whenever you evade.

#### Silent Whisper

While a spell with the Silent Whisper cantrip is prepared, you may telepathically whisper a short message to one creature that you are aware of within 5 paces. The target may not respond.

#### Uncanny Insight

While you have a spell with the Uncanny Insight cantrip prepared, all your Intuition checks against another intelligent creature are easier as long as you maintain eye contact with that creature.

## First-Circle Arcane Spells

### Arcane Bolts

*Aetheric Conjuration*  

*Cantrip - Magic Missile (Aetheric)*

When you cast this spell, a number of motes of aetheric energy appear around you equal to half your level (round up). While you focus, they circle you slowly at a distance of two feet; they do not emit light to see by, but can be seen from up to 20 paces away as if they emitted dim light.

**Fast -** At any time thereafter, you may fire one of these motes at a creature that you can see within 10 paces. The mote passes through all non-aetheric barriers on its way to its target, inflicting 1d6 aetheric damage.

### Arcane Mark

*Aetheric Divination*  

*Cantrip - Detect Magic*

**Fast -** You touch a small or tiny inanimate object and an invisible rune appears on it, visible only through the Identify Magic or Arcane Sight spells, or similar effects. While you maintain focus, you know precisely the distance and direction to the object, and its approximate speed and direction of travel.

### Beguile

*Psychic Transmutation*  

*Cantrip - Friends*

You attempt to beguile an intelligent mortal creature within 5 paces that makes eye contact with you or can hear your voice. If it saves vs. Trick, it knows that you attempted to manipulate its mind, and its attitude likely degrades by one step. If it fails, its attitude towards you becomes Friendly for as long as you focus. The effect immediately ends if you do anything harmful to the beguiled creature; if they suffer injury from any other source, they also immediately make a saving throw to attempt to snap out of it.

### Continual Light

*Flame Conjuration*  

*Cantrip - Flickering Glow*

While you maintain focus, your hand or one held object emits bright light out to 5 paces and dim light out to 10 paces. If you touch the light directly to a creature and succeed in a melee attack, it suffers 1d6 radiant damage and is blinded.

### Darkness

*Flame Conjuration*  

*Cantrip - Shroud*

While you maintain focus, you snuff out all light within a 5 pace radius. Any light that is further than 5 paces away, but within 10 paces, is dimmed by one step - bright light becomes dim light, dim light becomes deep shadow, and deep shadow becomes pitch blackness. A supernaturally produced light that is being actively maintained by another magic-user may be able to pierce the darkness if the caster saves vs. Trick; this saving throw is easier if the other caster is higher level than you, or hard if they are below half your level.

### Feather Fall

*Air Transmutation*  

*Cantrip - Jump*

**Fast -** You can cast this spell on a creature within 10 paces. While you focus, its rate of descent drops to 50 feet per round whenever it would fall, and it always lands on its feet and takes no falling damage.

### Floating Disc

*Aetheric Conjuration*  

*Cantrip - Mage Hand*

You may conjure a circular, horizontal plane of force in the shape of a cupped disc 3 feet in diameter, that floats 3 feet above the ground in an unoccupied space of your choice that you can see within range. The disc remains while you maintain focus, and can hold up to 500 pounds. If more weight is placed on it, the spell ends, and everything on the disc falls to the ground.

The disc is immobile while you are within 5 paces of it, following you within 5 paces as you move further away. It ignores difficult terrain, but it can’t cross elevation changes of 10 feet or more, and the spell ends if it is ever more than 20 paces away.

### Shield

*Aetheric Abjuration*  

*Cantrip - Resistance (physical or aetheric)*

**Fast -** You can cast this spell with a free hand in reaction to a weapon attack or similar physical non-elemental injury, or by any effect that deals aetheric damage.

An invisible bubble of aetheric energy forms around you, absorbing some of the damage and shielding you from harm. Roll 2d6 and gain that many temporary hit points.

While any hit points are remaining, you halve all damage that you receive from weapons or aetheric effects.

### Silent Image

*Psychic Conjuration*  

*Cantrip - Shroud*

You create the image of an object, a creature, or some other visible phenomenon that is no larger than a 15-foot cube. The image appears at a spot within 10 paces and lasts for the duration. The image is purely visual; it isn’t accompanied by sound, smell, or other sensory effects. Physical interaction with the image reveals it to be an illusion, because things can pass through it.

While you maintain focus, you can cause the image to move to any spot within range. As the image changes location, you can alter its appearance so that its movements appear natural for the image.

You can also use this spell to make yourself and your clothing and equipment look different. To discern that you are disguised, a creature can inspect your appearance and make a saving throw.

### Sleep

*Psychic Transmutation*  

*Cantrip - Elemental Grasp (Psychic)*

You make eye contact with another living creature or creatures within 5 paces of you whose total levels are less than twice your own. Each affected creature is stunned for one combat round, then must save vs Curse or fall unconscious for 1d6 exploration turns. A creature higher than your own level rolls an easy saving throw; a creature less than half your own level rolls a hard saving throw.

Once asleep, an affected creature cannot wake on its own, and any attempt to rouse the creature is hard.

### Subtle Weave

*Aetheric Abjuration*  

*Cantrip - Spell Ward*

This spell does not require any somatic or verbal aspects. While you maintain focus, you can attempt to silently cast another prepared spell without its normal somatic aspects.

Make a hard Stealth check; if you succeed, the other spell is cast without needing to speak or move. If you fail, you lose focus on this spell, and lose the other prepared spell without casting it.

## Second-Circle Arcane Spells

### Arcane Sight

*Aetheric Divination*  

*Cantrip - Detect Magic*

While you maintain focus, you can see any invisible creatures, supernatural effects, or magical auras as glowing shapes. You may focus your attention on an individual item or creature, gaining detailed information as if casting Identify Magic on it.

### Arcane Lock

*Aetheric Abjuration*  

*Cantrip - Mage Hand*

You touch a closed door, window, gate, chest, or other entryway or container with an empty hand, and it becomes locked until this effect is dispelled. All attempts to pick the lock or force it open are hard. You may choose a password when you cast this spell; speaking the password will release the lock and open the entryway for ten minutes.

### Comprehend

*Psychic Divination*  

*Cantrip - Uncanny Insight*

While you focus, you understand the literal meaning of any spoken language that you hear while you make eye contact with the speaker. You also understand the meaning of any written language that you see, but you must be touching the surface on which the words are written. Encoded messages and secret glyphs are not decoded by this spell.

### Detect Illusions

*Psychic Divination*  

*Cantrip - Uncanny Insight*

You close your eyes and then open them to take in your surroundings. If there are any supernatural illusion effects affecting your perceptions, you will notice them as a slight shimmer in your vision and a tingling sensation on the back of your neck.

This spell will not let you pierce the illusion or see what is actually present; it will only alert you that there is, in fact, some kind of effect obscuring or altering your true perceptions.

### Elemental Ray

*Elemental Conjuration*  

*Cantrip - Magic Missile (elemental)*

You fire a ray of energy, of an element chosen during preparation. Make a ranged attack throw that deals 2d6 elemental damage at a range of 20 paces.

### Invisibility

*Psychic Transmutation*  

*Cantrip - Shroud*

You touch a creature or object. While you maintain focus, the target is invisible to normal sight and darkvision. Anything the target is wearing or carrying is invisible as long as it is on the target’s person. The spell ends if the target attacks or casts any other magic.

### Knock

*Earth Transmutation*  

*Cantrip - Elemental Grasp (Blunt)*

You rap your hand against a door, lock, or other portal. That portal instantly and permanently opens, destroying any mechanism that might allow it to close again. Doors or mechanisms with a structure rating might withstand this spell; such a door suffers 1D6 structure damage.

### Levitate

*Air Transmutation*  

*Cantrip - Jump*

You touch one creature or loose object of your choice that weighs less than 500 lbs. The target rises vertically, up to 20 feet, and remains suspended there while you maintain focus.

An unwilling creature may save vs. Hold against this effect.

The target can only move itself by pushing or pulling against a fixed object or surface within reach with an Agility check. You can move the target by up to 10 feet in any direction as your action, within the spell’s range. When the spell ends, the target falls to the ground.

### Locate Object

*Aetheric Divination*  

*Cantrip - Detect Magic*

Describe or name an object that is familiar to you. While you maintain focus, you sense the direction to the object’s location and its approximate distance, as long as that object is within one mile of you. If the object is in motion, you know the direction of its movement.

The spell can locate a specific object known to you, as long as you have seen it up close – within 5 paces – at least once. Alternatively, the spell can locate the nearest object of a particular kind, such as a certain kind of apparel, jewelry, furniture, tool, or weapon. This spell fails if any thickness of orichalc or lead, even a thin sheet, blocks a direct path between the caster and the object.

### Phantasm

*Psychic Conjuration*  

*Cantrip - Minor Image*

You create the image of an object, a creature, or some other phenomenon that is no larger than a 20-foot cube. It appears at a spot that you can see within 15 paces, and seems completely real, including any sounds, smells, or other appropriate sensations. A creature that closely examines it can save vs. Trick to notice that it is an illusion.

As long as you focus and stay within range of the illusion, you can cause it to move to any other spot within range, and you can alter its appearance and other sensory effects so that its movements, sounds, and so on appear natural for the image, such as making it appear to walk or even making it carry on a conversation.

### Read Thoughts

*Psychic Divination*  

*Cantrip - Uncanny Insight*

While you maintain focus, you can read the thoughts of certain creatures. When you cast the spell and as your action on each turn until the spell ends, you can detect the presence of thinking creatures within 5 paces of you with Intelligence of -2 or higher, even if you can’t see them. The spell can penetrate barriers, but 2 feet of rock, 2 inches of metal other than lead or orichalc, or a thin sheet of lead or orichalc blocks you. Once you detect the presence of a creature in this way, you can read its thoughts as described above even if you can’t see it while it is within 5 paces.

You can focus your mind on any one creature within 5 paces of you that you can see or detect with this spell. If the creature you choose has an Intelligence of -4 or lower or doesn’t speak any language, it is unaffected. You initially learn the surface thoughts of the creature you focus on — what is most on its mind in that moment. As an action, you can either shift your attention to another creature’s thoughts or attempt to probe deeper into the same creature’s mind. If you probe deeper, the target can resist with a save vs. Trick. Otherwise, you gain insight into its reasoning (if any), its emotional state, and something that looms large in its mind (such as something it worries over, loves, or hates). If you fail, the spell ends. Either way, the target knows that you are probing into its mind, and unless you shift your attention to another creature’s thoughts, the creature can use its action on its turn to make another saving throw to break the spell.

### Sending

*Psychic Conjuration*  

*Cantrip - Silent Whisper*

You silently send a message of 25 words or less to one creature that you are familiar with, regardless of distance. The target hears the message in their mind and recognizes you as the sender. As long as the target has an Intelligence of -3 or higher, they understand the meaning even if they do not normally speak your language.

## Third-Circle Arcane Spells

### Circle of Protection

*Aetheric Abjuration*  

*Cantrip - Spell Ward*

You sprinkle roughly an ounce of aetheric materia, ground iron dust, and ground feysilver dust in a 5-foot-diameter circle, pausing every few inches to inscribe appropriate runes and sigils, to create a 10foot-tall cylinder of magical energy. The ground dust that defines the circle glows with an eerie light while the spell remains active. If cast as a ritual, the spell creates a 10-foot radius circle which proscribes a 20-foot-tall cylinder. Celestial, elemental, fey, infernal, and undead creatures can’t willingly enter or leave the cylinder by non-magical means. If the creature tries to use teleportation or interplanar travel to do so, it must first succeed at a hard save vs. Curses. Each such creature also finds its attack throws against targets on the other side of the border are harder, and targets on the other side of the border can’t be beguiled, panicked, or possessed by such a creature.

Finally, all arcane magics within the circle are easier, and all non-arcane magics cast within or across the circle’s boundary are harder.

The circle lasts while you maintain focus, or until the next sunrise or sunset if cast as a ritual. It then fades into a dusty residue and a lingering faded or burned-in pattern where the circle was made.

### Clairvoyance

*Aetheric Divination*  

*Cantrip - Detect Magic*

You create an invisible sensor up to a mile away in a location familiar to you (a place you have visited or seen before) or in an obvious location up to 20 paces away that is unfamiliar to you (such as behind a door, around a corner, or in a grove of trees).

The sensor remains in place while you maintain focus, and it can’t be attacked or otherwise interacted with. You can choose to perceive all sight and sound through the sensor throughout the next round, as if you were standing in the sensor’s space. As you do, you may move the sensor up to 3 paces in any direction, including up or down.

### Cloud of Daggers

*Earth Conjuration*  

*Cantrip - Magic Missile (Earth)*

You fill the air with spinning, razor-sharp shards of crystal in a sphere two paces in radius, centered on a point you choose within range. Each creature within the area must save vs. blast, or take 1d6 piercing damage for every 2 levels of the caster (round up). Each damage die that rolls a ‘6’ inflicts ongoing bleeding damage to one target that failed its saving throw.

While you focus, each creature must save again or take 2d6 piercing damage when it starts its turn in the spell’s area or enters that area for the first time on a turn.

### Cone of Cold

*Water Conjuration*  

*Cantrip - Magic Missile (Cold)*

You blast a cone of freezing air 20 paces long from your hands that inflicts 1d6 cold damage per level of the caster. Each damage die that rolls a ‘6’ stuns and hobbles a creature of your choice that failed its saving throw.

### Darkvision

*Flame Divination*  

*Cantrip - Detect Magic*

While you maintain focus, you can see in perfect darkness out to 10 paces. You cannot distinguish colors or read fine detail without light, but can see the shape of any objects that are not invisible or obscured.

This spell projects a particularly bright 'beacon' of magical energy in a 10-pace cone; while this effect is invisible to mundane eyes, creatures that can see magic can spot it from a great distance.

### Dispel Magic

*Aetheric Abjuration*  

*Cantrip - Spell Ward*

**Fast -** You choose a magical effect on the target while touching them with your hand, then make a Science check to end that effect. You may also target any spell, miracle, or other magical effect that you can see within 25 paces as it is being cast. This roll is easy for an effect made by a creature or magic-user whose level is less than half your own, or hard if the effect is from a source whose level is higher than your own.

### Fireball

*Flame Conjuration*  

*Cantrip - Magic Missile (Fire)*

You fling a glowing red ember at a space within 20 paces, which detonates into a 4-pace radius fiery explosion that inflicts 1d6 fire damage per level of the caster. Any damage die that rolls a ‘6’ ignites a target of your choice that failed its saving throw, inflicting ongoing fire damage.

### Fly

*Air Transmutation*  

*Cantrip - Jump*

As your action while flying, you can dash up to 60 paces in a straight line rather than moving 15 paces; if you pass adjacent to any other creature or obstacle, you must take an Agility check as part of your action to avoid colliding with it.

### Hold Person

*Aetheric Abjuration*  

*Cantrip - Mage Hand*

As your action, choose a Large or smaller humanoid that you can see within 10 paces.

The target must save vs. Hold or be restrained for the duration. On each of its turns, the restrained creature can use its action to attempt to break free.

### Lightning Bolt

*Air Conjuration*  

*Cantrip - Magic Missile (Lightning)*

You gesture with your hands, and a stroke of lightning forming a line 20 paces long and 2 paces wide blasts out from your hands in a direction you choose, dealing 1d6 lightning damage per level of the caster to each creature along the line.

Creatures with metal armor or  weapons find their saving throw harder, and the lightning may ignite flammable objects in the area that aren’t being worn or carried. For each damage die that rolls a ‘6’, one affected creature of your choice that failed its saving throw becomes stunned, and is disarmed if it is wielding a metal weapon.

### Shelter

*Aetheric Abjuration*  

*Cantrip - Blade Ward*

A 10-foot-radius immobile dome of force springs into existence around and above you and remains stationary while you maintain focus and don't leave its radius. The sphere has 3d6 structure points, is resistant to all nonaetheric damage, and immune to all nonstructure damage. eight medium or small creatures can fit inside the dome with you.

The spell fails if its area includes a larger creature or more than nine creatures. Spells and other magical effects can't extend through the dome or be cast through it.

If cast as a ritual at sunrise or sunset, the dome lasts until the next sunrise or sunset without your focus so long as you remain within it, and all creatures and objects within the dome when you cast this spell can move through it freely. All other creatures and objects are barred from passing through it.

Until the spell ends, you can command the interior to become dimly lit or dark. The dome is opaque from the outside, of any color you choose, but it is transparent from the inside. The atmosphere inside the space is comfortable and dry, regardless of the weather outside.

### Suggestion

*Psychic Transmutation*  

*Cantrip - Friends*

You make eye contact with a target within 5 paces and suggest a belief or course of activity (no more than a sentence or two). Unless they save vs. Tricks, they must follow or believe your suggestion for as long as you continue to focus. Whenever they would be presented with evidence that contradicts your suggestion, they may make another saving throw to attempt to snap out of it.

This spell has no effect if the target cannot hear or understand you, and the target’s saving throw is easy if your command is obviously and immediately harmful to it.

*Cantrip - Detect Magic*

You cause an invisible, magical eye to form at your forehead. You mentally receive visual information from the eye when you close your own eyes, and it has darkvision out to 5 paces. The arcane eye can look in every direction, and can also see magical auras and creatures as if casting the Arcane Sight spell. You can move the eye up to 5 paces in any direction each round.

### Confusion

*Cantrip - Friends*

1 - The creature uses its action to make a melee attack

against a randomly determined creature within its reach, then

becomes stunned. If there is no creature within its reach, the

creature does nothing this round.

2, 3 - The creature uses all its movement to move in a

random direction. It then becomes stunned.

4, 5 - The creature is for stunned this round.

6 - The creature can act and move normally this round. At the

end of the round, it can save vs. Trick to attempt to snap out

of it; if it succeeds, the effect ends for this creature.

### Dimension Door

*Aetheric Conjuration*  

*Cantrip - Mage Hand*

You teleport it and all its carried equipment from its current location to any other spot that you can see within 100 paces. You can teleport yourself along with it, if desired.

If a creature would arrive in a location already occupied by an object or a creature, it takes 3d6 aetheric damage, and the spell fails to teleport it. If any damage dice rolled a ‘6’, that creature suffers a critical hit.

### Dominate

*Psychic Transmutation*  

*Cantrip - Friends*

You attempt to beguile a creature within 5 paces. If it fails its save vs. Trick, the target is beguiled for the duration. If the target is hostile to you, its saving throw is easier. Each time the target takes damage, it makes a new saving throw to end the spell.

While the target is beguiled, you have a telepathic link with it as long as the two of you are on the same plane of existence. You can use this telepathic link to issue commands to the creature while you are conscious (no action required), which it does its best to obey. You can specify a simple and general course of action, such as "attack that creature", "run over there", or "fetch that object". If the creature completes the order and doesn’t receive further direction from you, it defends and preserves itself to the best of its ability. You can take total and precise control of the target. Until the end of your next turn, the creature takes only the actions you choose, and doesn’t do anything that you don’t allow it to do.

### Elemental Wall

*Elemental Conjuration*  

*Cantrip - Elemental Grasp*

Each of these four spells conjures a wall of elemental energy: stone, ice, poisonous vapor, or flame. The wall is up to 10 feet high, and takes up a total of 50 contiguous squares that are each one pace by one pace wide; each space must be within 20 paces of you. Any creature caught within the wall's area must save vs. Blast to avoid its initial effects. While you maintain focus, the wall's elemental effects fill the chosen space:

**Wall of Stone -** a wall of stone blocks passage, and inflicts 4d6 blunt damage as it erects to each creature caught within any of its spaces. Those creatures are then forcibly ejected to the nearest non-wall space. Each space has 5 structure if attacked.

**Wall of Ice** - a wall of ice blocks passage. Any creature caught within it as it erects suffers 4d6 cold damage, and is immobilized and trapped within the ice if any damage die rolls a '6'; otherwise, that creature is forcibly ejected. Each space has 2 structure if attacked. **Wall of Miasma** - a wall of vapour obscures vision; each creature within or adjacent to the wall or passing through it becomes hobbled for one round, and must save vs. Doom or be stunned as they retch and gag from the fumes. **Wall of Flame** - a wall of flame inflicts 2d6 damage to any creature that passes through it, or 4d6 damage to any creature that starts its turn within it. If any damage dice roll a '6', that creature is lit on fire.

### Weave Element

*Elemental Abjuration*  

*Cantrip - Resistance (elemental)*

**Fast -** You can cast this spell when you would be damaged by an elemental effect or attack that matches the prepared element or its opposite *(air opposes earth, water opposes fire)*. If cast against an opposing element, ignore all damage from that element that you would receive from this effect or attack, and this spell immediately ends.

If cast against the same element, you halve all damage from that element immediately and for as long as you focus, and you also ignore one die of elemental damage from each attack or damage source that affects you.

For each die of elemental damage negated by this spell from any one source, you gain one die of elemental energy. If at any point you have more elemental energy dice than your level, or if you lose focus, this spell immediately ends and all remaining elemental energy dice are rolled as damage in a 5 pace wide blast centered on you. Creatures other than you can attempt to save for half damage.

While you have one or more elemental energy dice, if you would receive any damage from the opposing element, each elemental energy die cancels out one die of damage from that element. If this reduces your elemental energy dice to zero, this spell ends.

Finally, the next time you make a weapon attack or cast a damaging elemental spell of this spell's element while you have one or more elemental energy dice, you add all those elemental energy dice to that spell or attack’s damage roll and then end this spell. You also end this spell whenever an adjacent creature hits you with a melee attack, to automatically inflict those dice as damage to the attacker.

### Disintegrate

*Aetheric Transmutation*  

*Cantrip - Magic Missile (Aetheric)*

You direct an impossibly thin, vividly purplish-white beam of energy at a target within 25 paces as a ranged attack throw that inflicts 3D6 aetheric structure damage; if any damage dice roll a ‘6’, the attack is a critical hit. If reduced to 0 hp, the target is reduced to a fine grey powder.

If an object less than 5’ on a side is destroyed by this spell, it is also reduced to a fine grey powder. An object larger than a 5’ cube instead has a 5’ spherical hole vaporized into it at the point of impact.

### Flesh to Stone

*Earth Transmutation*  

*Cantrip - Elemental Grasp*

Make eye contact with a creature within 10 paces that can see you. If it saves vs. Hold, the spell ends and has no effect; otherwise, it is immobilized and suffers 3d6 petrification damage. While you maintain focus, it suffers this damage again at the end of each round.

The target can use its action to attempt to break free of the spell, ending the effect on a successful save vs. Hold.

A creature reduced to 0 hit points by the spell becomes fully petrified. Only a restoration miracle or dispel magic spell can undo the effect, returning them to life at zero hit points, in exactly the condition they were in when petrified.

### Giant Fist

*Aetheric Conjuration*  

*Cantrip - Mage Hand*

You conjure a Large construct of a giant floating hand that mirrors the motions of your own hand for as long as you maintain focus.

The hand has a Strength and Constitution of +5, and a Dexterity equal to your Intelligence. Melee attacks with the hand are easy. It has 2D6 structure points, and all non-aetheric damage dealt to it from any source is halved. You may use the hand as your action to attack, push, grasp, or block, moving it up to 5 paces in any direction as you do so. If you perform any other action, the hand moves alongside you as you move.

**Attack** - You direct the hand to make a melee attack. If you hit, the hand inflicts 5d6 aetheric damage, or 1D6 structural aetheric damage.

**Push** - You direct the hand to push a target up to 2d6 paces in a direction that you choose, unless it saves vs. knockdown. The target’s saving throw is hard if it is medium-sized or smaller, and easy if it is a huge creature.

**Grasp** - You direct the hand to make a melee attack, which is easy if the target is Medium or smaller. On a success, the target is grappled, but can attempt to break free as its action with a save vs. knockdown — this saving throw is hard for medium or smaller creatures, and easy for huge creatures. While you have a target grappled, you may use your action each round to crush it, inflicting 5d6 blunt damage or 1D6 structural blunt damage. **Block** - The hand automatically moves itself between you and a creature of your choice, and will continue to do so as a free action until you give it a different command. You gain half cover, and the target must save vs knockdown to attempt to move through its space. If it succeeds, the space is still considered difficult terrain. Any ranged attack that passes through the hand’s space is harder to hit, and any blast, cone, or ray that the hand shelters a creature from is easier for that creature to avoid.

### Resilient Sphere

*Aetheric Abjuration*  

*Cantrip - Resistance (all damage)*

A sphere of shimmering force encloses a creature or object of Large size or smaller within 10 paces. If any unwilling creature is within the area, each such creature must successfully save vs. Blast and dodge, or be enclosed until the sphere is destroyed.

While you maintain focus, the sphere has 3d6 structure points, is resistant to all non-aetheric damage, and immune to all non-structure damage. Nothing – not physical objects, energy, or other spell effects – can pass through the barrier, in or out. While the sphere remains, a creature or object inside can’t be damaged by attacks or effects originating from outside, nor can a creature inside the sphere damage anything outside it. Any attempt to fire in or out automatically deals its damage to the sphere itself, rather than passing through.

The sphere is weightless and just large enough to contain the creature or object inside. An enclosed creature can use its action to push against the sphere’s walls and thus roll the sphere at up to half the creature’s speed. Similarly, the globe can be picked up and moved by other creatures.

Any damaging spell targeting the globe that destroys it does not harm anything inside it; the globe perfectly absorbs and dissipates all remaining energy as it shatters.

### Summon Elemental

*Elemental Conjuration*  

*Cantrip - Magic Missile (elemental)*

You summon an Elemental spirit to cross the veil into the Material and aid you and your allies in battle. The Elemental is a 5hd creature with +0 in all abilities; it automatically returns to the Elemental Chaos once either its hit points are depleted, or when the duration of its summoning ends.

Upon summoning, the elemental makes a saving throw; if the Elemental successfully saves, it immediately goes free and is likely to be hostile to the summoner. Otherwise, the Elemental is bound to do the Wizard’s bidding until the next sunrise or sunset, after which it is automatically banished back to its home on the Elemental Chaos.

The Elemental is controlled by the referee; it may cast any elemental spell or glamour that matches its element by spending 1d6 of its hit points per circle.

### Telepathic Bond

*Psychic Divination*  

*Cantrip - Uncanny Insight*

With an action, you can link the minds of a number of different adjacent willing creatures up to 1d6 + your Intelligence. While you maintain focus, each creature in the link can sense the emotions of each other creature, and can communicate telepathically whether or not they share a common language, regardless of distance.

If performed as a ritual, this spell links the minds of all participants until the next sunrise or sunset.
