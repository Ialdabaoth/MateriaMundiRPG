# Bard

### ()

*Fey Expert*  

### Class Features

#### Prime Ability:

*Charisma*  

#### Hit Die:

*d6+Con*  

#### Recommended Starting Equipment

Two daggers, quarterstaff, medium armor, musical instrument

#### Martial Training

Bards are not familiar with any non-simple weapons or armor.

#### Talent Training

Each time you gain a level, you gain training in your choice of **Finesse**, **Lore**, **Charm,** or **Wit**. At levels 5 and 9, you may instead gain training in any one talent of your choice.

#### Bardsong

Starting at second level, whenever you focus entirely on singing or playing music, you can weave any Dream or Song glamour effect into the performance with a circle up to half your level (round down). Playing a bardsong requires a number of successful **Charm** checks equal to the glamour's circle; each check takes one round.

#### Magical Intuition

You may make a **Lore** check to attempt to activate any magic item or prepared magic, regardless of origin. Common items are easy to activate, while rare items are hard; prepared magic of a circle less than half your level is at normal difficulty, while prepared magic of a circle up to your level is at hard difficulty.
