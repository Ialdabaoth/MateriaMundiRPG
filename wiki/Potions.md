## Potions and Tinctures

Alchemical potions and tinctures are not meant to be ingested; instead, they are either thrown like bombs, or applied to an object or surface.

### Earth-Binding Potion

*Uncommon Potion*  

*Ingredients - Mimic mucous, Earth materia*

When thrown at a creature capable of flight, this potion entangles and fouls whatever apparatus they use to stay airborne, pulling them to the ground. A creature affected by this spell immediately loses their flying speed, and if flying, plummets to the ground.

### Potion of Elemental Fury

*Common Potion*  

*Ingredients - saltpetre, charcoal, Any Elemental*

*materia*

This potion may be thrown or shattered at your feet. When released, it unleashes a blast area attack with a radius of 2 paces, dealing 2d6 elemental damage.

When brewed as an Uncommon or Rare potion, the blast radius extends by 1 pace per rarity level, and the damage increases by 2d6 per rarity level.

### Tincture of Elemental Essence

*Rare Potion*  

*Ingredients - olive oil, any Elemental materia*

This potion produces a thick gel that is catalyzed by a small spark or a sharp blow to release a burst of elemental energy.

When coated onto an arrow or other projectile, the missile deals an additional +1d6 elemental damage on a hit the first time it is fired. If it misses, the elemental damage is unleashed onto the environment wherever the missile comes to rest.

When coated onto a melee weapon, the weapon deals +1d6 elemental damage each time it hits. A weapon coated with this tincture is protected from the effects of the imbued element, even if it would normally be vulnerable to that damage type. It will continue to inflict this extra damage until its elemental damage die rolls a '1', at which time the effects end.

### Potion of Entanglement

*Common Potion*  

*Ingredients - crawling Creeper seed pods, Wood*

*materia*

Grasping vines sprout from the ground, centered where this potion is poured or shattered. A circle 5 paces in diameter becomes difficult terrain, and any creature passing through the zone must make a Strength saving throw to avoid being restrained by the entangling plants until the effect ends. The vines last 1d6 combat rounds before withering into brittle, dry dust.

When brewed as an Uncommon potion, the vines grow sharp, hidden thorns, inflicting 1d6 piercing damage per pace of movement to each creature that attempts to move into or through the area, and the effect’s duration extends to minutes instead of rounds. A Rare potion lasts hours, or can produce a permanent hedge of thorny vines if the potion used to create it was imbued with sufficient Materia to preserve indefinitely.

### Potion of Faerie Fire

*Common Potion*  

*Ingredients -  firefly viscera, Flame materia*

When this potion shatters, the splashing liquid reacts with the air to begin glowing, coating everything within a 2 pace radius of the point of impact that fails a save vs. Blast. The effect lasts 1d6 combat rounds; all affected objects and creatures are easier to hit with attack throws, can’t benefit from invisibility, and emit dim light out to 10 feet.

### Potion of Bottled Fog

*Common Potion*  

*Ingredients - saltpetre, sugar, Water materia, Air*

*Materia*

Thick whitish-grey fog pours from this potion as it bubbles and evaporates, heavily obscuring everything it reaches. A circle 5 paces in diameter becomes heavily obscured.

The fog lasts 2d6 combat rounds unless blown away by a strong wind or heavy rain.

When brewed as an Uncommon potion, the area of effect increases to a circle 7 paces in diameter, and lasts 2d6 exploration turns.

At Rare, the area increases to 10 paces in diameter and lasts 1d6 hours.

### Bottle of Illusory Visions

*Common Potion*  

*Ingredients - morning glory seeds, magic*

*mushrooms, rye ergot, Illusion or Fey materia*

A Vision in a Bottle is a Silent Image spell dissolved into an alchemical solution. Upon uncorking the bottle and pouring out the potion, it immediately evaporates into mist, forming the illusion crafted while brewing.

The illusion lasts 1d6 hours. Unlike a normal Silent Image spell, neither the brewer nor the un-corker may alter or control the image once it is formed.

An Uncommon Bottle of Illusory Visions instead casts the Major Image spell, and a Rare bottle instead casts the Mirage spell.

### Magic Ink

*Common Potion*  

*Ingredients - Oak galls, orichalc dust, feysilver*

*dust, materia (each makes a different Ink)*

Magic Ink is the basic formula for the inks and dyes used to prepare arcane spells and scrolls, as well as divine prayer strips. While they all use the same formula, each different element requires its own mixture of magic ink, made with the proper materia to resonate with the appropriate element. There are seven distinct ‘colors’ of magic ink, each determined by the type of raw materia used and the specific process used to refine it:

- Aetheric ink is a deep, vibrant purple.

- Illusion ink is milky and opalescent

- Earth ink is rich bronze with flakes of

silver

- Water ink is deep blue, flecked with

turquoise and sea-green.

- Wood ink is a vivid green, with flashes of

gold and magenta.

- Air ink is a cloudy grey, occasionally shot

through with gold or light blue.

- Flame ink is a vibrant red-orange, with

flakes of gold and silver.

When a wizard brews magic ink, they roll 1d6 to determine the number of bottles of ink that are produced from the materia. Each bottle can prepare six total circles of Arcane magic spells, or three circles worth of spell scrolls.

### Salve of Slipperiness

*Common Potion*  

*Ingredients - hagfish slime, mineral oil, Water*

*materia*

This potion can cover approximately 100 square feet of surface. If poured out onto the ground, it covers a square or circle roughly 2 paces on a side. This patch lasts 1d6 combat rounds.

Each creature within the affected area must succeed on a hard Agility check or fall prone. The entire area becomes difficult terrain for the duration of the effect; any creature that enters or leaves the patch must also immediately roll Agility or fall prone.

### Sovereign Glue

*Uncommon Potion*  

*Ingredients - Mimic mucous, almonds, cotton,*

*aqua fortis, Earth and Wood materia*

Sovereign glue is an intensely sticky and fastdrying substance that can instantly and permanently weld any two surfaces together.

One brew is sufficient to stick together approximately one square foot of surface.

Once bonded, only a Salve of Slipperiness can undo the effect.

### Artificial Dragon Pearl

*Common+ Potion*  

*Ingredients - Elemental materia, aqua regia*

Creating a dream pearl or dragon pearl from harvested materia is an alchemical process that takes one day for a common pearl, one week for an uncommon pearl, one lunar month for a rare pearl, or one year for a legendary pearl. 50 charges of raw materia must be fed into the process each day, while the solution itself is kept at precise temperatures and pressures. At the end of the process, a large raw crystal of purified materia is extracted from the solution and painstakingly carved into a shape suitable for conducting the elemental, aetheric, or psychic energies.

Each day of brewing requires a Science check with a difficulty determined by the quality of the workshop and raw materials, and carving the final gem requires a Finesse test with a difficulty based on the pearl’s rarity - easy for a common pearl, normal for an uncommon pearl, or hard for a rare or legendary pearl. A single failure in this process ruins the entire attempt, wasting all time and materia consumed up to that point.
