## NPC Creation

Sometimes you find yourself needing to quickly generate a party of adventurers for a pick-up game, or rapidly creating an NPC during an adventure.

The following method reduces the entire character creation process to a single d20 roll. Set any Prime abilities to +1, and all other abilities to +0.

| D20 | Class | Origin | Prime(s) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1-3 | Warrior | Human | Str |
| 4-6 | Scout | Human | Dex |
| 7-8 | Wizard | Human | Int |
| 9-10 | Priest | Human | Wis |
| 11 | Champion | Human | Str |
| 12 | Hunter | Human | Dex |
| 13 | Druid | Human | Wis |
| 14 | Stalwart | Dwarf | Con and Str |
| 15 | Delver | Dwarf | Con and Wis |
| 16 | Dweomersmith | Dwarf | Con and Int |
| 17 | Hunter | Elf | Dex and Wis |
| 18 | Druid | Elf | Dex and Wis |
| 19 | Hunter | Gnome | Dex and Cha |
| 20 | Champion | Beastfolk | Dex and Str |

If you have four players and want to begin even faster, simply create a warrior, a scout, a wizard, and a priest, assigning their prime requisite to +1 and each of their other abilities to +0. This particular combination is probably the most classically stereotypical starting party, with a well-rounded set of feats and proficiencies for whatever challenges you want to throw at them.

### Gear

A PC or NPC should generally have the necessary gear to do their job, unless lacking it is part of the unfolding story. If you want to equip an NPC with magic items, you can roll on the Random Magic Item table and see if anything looks appropriate. A noteworthy 2-3 hd NPC will likely have at least one common magic item, while a 4-6 hd NPC might have an uncommon item, and a 7-9 hd NPC might have two uncommon items or even a rare item.

### Background and History

Populating your world with entertaining and believable non-player characters can be challenging for some referees. You can always start with inspiration from various media (Appendix N is a useful resource here), then make sufficient changes to suit the story and setting.

Many important NPCs even begin life as minions who manage to distinguish themselves somehow in an encounter. Don’t be afraid to use an amusing mishap or circumstance to promote a random goblin thief from an extra in a warband into a recurring nemesis for the party.

### Personality and Attitude

If you’re feeling uninspired or just want to create a personality randomly, you can use lists like the following whenever a new NPC shows up. You can roll a D6 to determine which trait column to use, then roll a second D6 to determine the trait from that column to apply. If you want more traits, you can roll again to pick a new column, stopping once you roll a column that has already been rolled.

| D6 | Trait 1 | Trait 2 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Forgiving | Naive |
| 2 | Kind | Optimistic |
| 3 | Honorable | Hopeful |
| 4 | Cowardly | Doubtful |
| 5 | Cruel | Pessimistic |
| 6 | Vindictive | Cynical |
| D6 | Trait 3 | Trait 4 |
| 1 | Selfless | Humble |
| 2 | Generous | Encouraging |
| 3 | Fair | Respectful |
| 4 | Shrewd | Condescending |
| 5 | Selfish | Boastful |
| 6 | Greedy | Arrogant |
| D6 | Trait 5 | Trait 6 |
| 1 | Cowardly | Bitter |
| 2 | Wary | Stern |
| 3 | Prudent | Reserved |
| 4 | Honorable | Excitable |
| 5 | Courageous | Gentle |
| 6 | Foolhardy | Sweet |
