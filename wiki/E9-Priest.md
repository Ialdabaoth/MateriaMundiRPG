*Magic-user class (divine).* Priests perform miracles through prayer and faith.

## Class Features

- **Prime ability:** Charisma
- **Hit die:** d6+Con
- **Recommended starting equipment:** [Quarterstaff](E9-Weapons#quarterstaff), [medium armor](E9-Armor-and-Shields#medium-armor), prayer book, holy symbol, dungeoneer's kit

## Talent Training

At levels 2 and 6, you gain training in [Intuition](E9-Talents#intuition); at levels 4 and 8, you gain training in [Bearing](E9-Talents#bearing).

## Domain of Faith

All priests choose a domain of faith, usually by joining a holy Order dedicated to that domain. You may perform the miracles associated with your domain, as well as any common miracle, using any prayer strip of the same or higher circle, regardless of what miracle was originally prepared into it.

## Divine Miracles

You may prepare miracles into prayer strips at sunrise and sunset, which you can use to perform their associated miracles. Once a miracle is performed, its prayer strip burns to ash.

### Preparing During Exploration

You can prepare during exploration, if you have an empty preparation of a suitable circle. This takes a number of [Bearing](E9-Talents#bearing) checks equal to the miracle's circle; each roll takes one exploration turn spent in intense prayer.

### Rebuke the Unholy

You use your action to flourish a holy symbol and make a Bearing check to attempt to drive back every unholy creature within a 5-pace cone that is your level or lower. You may instead attempt to drive back a single creature that is higher level, but your Bearing check is harder. Each affected creature functionally becomes [panicked](E9-Conditions#panicked) for as long as you focus, even if it is normally immune to fear-based effects. If you are fifth level or higher, any affected targets with only one hit die are destroyed or banished, instead of driven back. At ninth level, affected targets with two hit dice are destroyed as well.

### Divine Rituals

You can also perform miracles from your prayer book as divine rituals; each ritual takes one exploration turn per circle. You may perform any ritual from any domain, up to a circle of half your level (round up). Each round that you perform a ritual requires a Bearing check, or the ritual fails. If successful, a ritual's effect happens on the next exploration turn after it is completed.

### Sworn Oaths

Divine blessings can bestow semi-permanent effects on anyone who swears and upholds a particular oath as the ritual is performed. Any blessing requiring focus to maintain can instead be maintained via sworn oath. Violating a sacred oath immediately reverses the effects of the blessing, turning it into a curse that can only be removed once you have atoned for the transgression.

## Healing

Priests have access to healing magic directly. See [Healing Magic and Potions](E9-Resting-and-Healing#healing-magic-and-potions).

## Bonus Experience

Priests gain additional experience through accomplishing the goals of their faith. See [Class-Based Experience](E9-Advancement#magic-users).
