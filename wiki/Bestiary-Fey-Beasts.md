## Fey Beasts

Many strange creatures are native to Faerie, created by its chaotic energies as strange echoes of mortal beasts. Most such creatures are chimerae, displaying a mixture of features from different “natural” animals. Centaurs and griffins are both examples of kinds of creatures that began as chimerae, but had sufficiently sensible internal organs that they could breathe, eat, and even breed in the mortal world.

**Iron Vulnerability** - The fey are vulnerable to cold iron – unalloyed lodestone, telluric iron, or meteoric iron that has never been heated enough to glow by mortal means. Damage to a fey beast from any cold iron weapon also stuns them, causes all saving throws to be harder until it heals, and prevents it from using any of its special feats until healed. **Glamour -** Fey beasts retain a spark of Faerie’s power, even in the mortal world. Fey beasts can often weave primal magic more readily than a druid, using materia in their own body to power their glamours. Areas in the material world that have been exposed to elemental energies will often see the offspring of natural animals mutating into fey beasts, as residual energies diffuse into the environment.

All fey beasts have a pool of glamour equal to their level which they can use to power their special feats, sometimes including primal magics. A fey beast uses and regains glamour as a druid: A first-circle glamour costs 1 point from its pool, a second-circle glamour costs 2 points, and a third-circle glamour costs 3. A fey beast can regain one glamour by resting and making a Survival check.

### Fey Beasts in Their Natural Environment

While fey beasts are supernatural creatures, they integrate fully into the ecosystem of the material plane, breeding and hunting and being preyed upon like any other creature.

Their presence puts serious pressure on any ecosystem they live in, however, making it obvious when they have made a particular area their home.

Noticing that a one-league hex is the territory of a fey beast is a Survival check, which can be made in secret on behalf of the party's guide by the referee when they first enter the hex. Determining what kind of fey beasts inhabit a hex requires deliberate investigation.

### Fey Beast Intelligence

Most fey beasts have the intelligence of a very cunning natural animal, but a few fey beasts, such as unicorns, are intelligent creatures that are often capable of speech. These creatures either choose to live in harmony with nature, or simply lack the capacity or desire to use tools and integrate with mortal society. An intelligent fey beast is still considered a beast, rather than a mortal, for magical effects that care about such matters.

### Fey Beasts as Animal Companions

Druids capable of casting Beast Bond can attempt to "train" a fey beast as an animal companion, but such creatures are far more willful and temperamental than natural animals. Hunters can also try to train a fey beast if they acquire them young enough - ideally immediately after birth. Raising a fey beast will be an arduous montage of hard Survival checks and minor quests to keep it fed and healthy until it reaches maturity.

An intelligent fey beast is another matter, since it has its own will and desires and intellect. They are negotiated with and brought on as hirelings using Bearing, rather than Survival, even though they count as beasts in all other respects. An intelligent fey beast will often have quite unusual demands, compared to other allies or hirelings.

### Barrow-Cat

*(Level 3)*  

A barrow-cat is a large feline the size of a leopard with large, luminous eyes and a wide face with an oversized grinning mouth.

Exceptionally long and prehensile tendrils extend from either side of its face like a pair of animated whiskers, each terminating in a ‘tuft’ of fur and true whiskers. The most common coloring for a barrow-cat is black, often with a white ‘star’ pattern on its chest.
```
Doom 11+ Hold 9+ Blast 8+ Trick 14+ Curse 11+ Fear 10+
Armor:         1               HP:    3d6+3
Movement:      Very Fast       Size:  Medium
Attacks (9+):  bite (2d6+3 piercing), claws (2d6+3)
Special Feat:  A barrow-cat can use its action to become
invisible until it attacks. While invisible, it may cast a major
illusion of itself up to 5 paces away.
Con +1 Str +3  Dex +4 Int -2   Wis +1 Cha  +2
Behavior (d6): 1 curious, 2-5 wary, 6 aggressive
Talents (3+):  Agility, Alertness, Stealth, Survival
Senses:        Hearing (superior)
```
