## Elixirs

Elixirs invariably have a positive effect when imbibed. A particular elixir can be identified by a hard Medicine roll.

### Elixir of Antivenin

*Uncommon Elixir*  

*Ingredients - giant spider venom, ephedra stalk,*

*Wood materia*

An Elixir of Antivenin immediately neutralizes any poisons affecting the imbiber, then halves all poison damage they receive and makes all saves vs. poison effects easy for the next 2d6 combat rounds. When the effect wears off, the imbiber suffers 2d6 damage.

### Barkskin Elixir

*Uncommon Elixir*  

*Ingredients - ironwood bark, Wood materia*

An Elixir of Barkskin hardens the imbiber’s skin to the consistency of wooden bark. For the next hour, they gain an Armor of 2, but all Alertness checks and saving throws that involve touch become harder. After the elixir wears off, the imbiber continues to suffer this disadvantage, and is mildly distracted by the bark itching and flaking off of their skin, causing all their actions to be slower. This aftereffect remains until they complete a full night’s sleep.

### Elixir of Darkvision

*Uncommon Elixir*  

*Ingredients - Giant spider eyes, Flame materia*

An Elixir of Darkvision grants the imbiber natural darkvision for its duration. Roll 1d6 to determine the number of hours the effect lasts. When the effect wears off, the imbiber must save or be blinded until they sleep for at least 6 hours. Even if they succeed, all Alertness tests and saving throws vs. illusion involving vision are harder until they complete a full night’s sleep.

### Elixir of Enhanced Ability

*Elixir*  

*Ingredients - Wood materia, and various*

*ingredients that depend on the ability to be*

*enhanced.*

An Elixir of Enhanced Ability significantly increases one of the imbiber’s ability scores. The imbiber gains a temporary static bonus to the ability chosen when brewing the potion, which is based on the elixir’s rarity:

| Rarity | Bonus |
|---|---|
| Uncommon | +1 |
| Rare | +2 |
| Legendary | +3 |

A character may not have two static bonuses to the same ability; if any other effect other than natural ability score or species would grant that creature a bonus to the same ability as this elixir, only the higher bonus applies. If you gain a full +3 bonus to an ability above its natural value, all task tests using talents linked to that ability also become easier.

This bonus decreases by 1 per exploration turn until it becomes a -1 penalty, which remains until the imbiber sleeps for at least 6 hours. While you have any penalty to an ability, all task tests using talents linked to that ability are harder.

### Elixir of Giant Growth

*Rare Elixir*  

*Ingredients - Acorn, Wood materia*

An Elixir of Giant Growth increases the imbiber’s size by one size level - small creatures become medium, medium creatures become large, large creatures become huge, and so on. This grants them all the normal benefits and drawbacks of being one size class larger. The imbiber also gains a +3 bonus to Strength and Constitution, and suffers -1 to their Dexterity. Clothing and gear are unfortunately unaffected; any worn clothing or armor will need to be salvaged and repaired. The imbiber must immediately save or become stunned as they readjust to their new size.

The effect lasts 2d6 combat rounds; the referee will roll the duration in secret. After the effect wears off, the imbiber suffers a critical hit.

### Elixir of Haste

*Rare Elixir*  

*Ingredients - Coca leaves, coffee beans, cacao*

*beans, cinnamon bark, ephedra stalk, Wood*

*materia, Air materia, Flame materia*

An Elixir of Haste grants the imbiber two full additional actions per round for as long as it lasts. One action is fast, one is at normal speed, and one is slow, regardless of the normal speed of each action. Each action includes normal movement.

This effect lasts 1d6 combat rounds; the imbiber then suffers a critical hit.

### Elixir of Health

*Elixir*  

*Ingredients - Ginseng root, giant bee honey,*

*orange blossoms, Wood materia*

A common elixir of health allows a character to roll half of their hit dice (round up) and recover half that many hit points (round up again), with no negative aftereffects. An uncommon elixir of health allows a character to roll all their hit dice, then recover that many hit points.

### Longstrider Elixir

*Common Elixir*  

*Ingredients - Coca leaves, snake oil, Wood materia*

A Longstrider Elixir infuses the imbiber with vital energy, invigorating their leg muscles and tendons. For 2d6 combat rounds, their base speed and dash speed are doubled and their jumping distance is tripled. The imbiber then suffers 1d6 damage and is hobbled until they heal if they fail their saving throw.

### Elixir of Prophylaxis

*Uncommon Elixir*  

*Ingredients - Blue bread mold, willow bark, Wood*

*materia*

An Elixir of Prophylaxis immediately grants the imbiber one saving throw against any disease condition affecting them, while also making all such saving throws easier until the next sunrise or sunset. However, all attack throws, ability checks, and task checks become harder for the same duration.

### Elixir of Regeneration

*Rare Elixir*  

*Ingredients - Axolotl blood, gecko tail, bamboo*

*shoots, Wood materia*

An Elixir of Regeneration drastically increases the imbiber’s natural healing abilities, allowing wounds to close and heal before their very eyes. The effect lasts 2d6 combat rounds; at the end of each of those rounds, the imbiber heals one hit die worth of damage. As damage heals, the imbiber may make a reflexive Constitution save each round for one of their lasting injury effects chosen at random; on a success, that effect is downgraded to its lower version if it was a serious lasting injury, or removed altogether if it was a minor lasting injury.

This potion can even regrow lost limbs or sensory organs, if given enough time. If poured into the mouth or wounds of a corpse that has been dead for fewer hours than one roll of its hit die, it makes a saving throw vs. Doom. On success, it returns to life and immediately begins regenerating. If the corpse is in pieces, the head must be attached to or adjacent to the neck, and the upper body must contain enough heart tissue to begin beating. Once the effects of the potion wear off, the imbiber is incapable of healing by any means until they sleep for at least 6 hours.

### Elixir of Water Breathing

*Uncommon Elixir*  

*Ingredients - lungfish bladder, Water materia, Air*

*materia*

The Elixir of Water Breathing allows the imbiber to breathe both in air and underwater. The potion lasts a number of exploration turns equal to 1d6 + the imbiber’s Constitution. After the effect wears off, the imbiber suffers 2d6 damage.
