# Materia Mundi SRD

Welcome to the Materia Mundi SRD wiki! It holds two rulesets:

- **[E9 System Reference Handbook](E9)**: the free, open rules core (CC BY-SA 4.0). Two kinds of dice, abilities from −3 to +3, d20 attacks and saves, d6 talent checks, and nine levels. Its pages are named `E9-…`.
- **[Materia Mundi: Compendium](Compendium)**: the full game built on E9, with eleven classes, the Panoply of gear and magic items, arcane spells, miracles, dweomers and glamours, a Bestiary, the Referee's Guide and the appendix tables.

## Linking to this wiki

Page names and section anchors here are stable, so you can link straight to a rule, item, spell or creature: for example `Weapons#longsword`, `Arcane-Spells#fireball`, `Bestiary-Animals#giant-rat` or `E9-Conditions#hobbled`. Every page ends with the book pages it comes from.
