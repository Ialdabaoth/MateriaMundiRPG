You are encumbered if you are wearing too much gear or carrying too heavy a load.

## Carrying Capacity

Your maximum carrying capacity is equal to **6 + your Strength** ability.

## Weight

| Item | Weight |
|---|---|
| Heavy weapon | 1 |
| Shield | 1 |
| Armor | Equal to its defense (light 1, medium 2, heavy 3) |
| Each 25 lb of other gear or treasure | 1 |
| Oversized weapon or gear (a polearm, a 10' pole) | +1 beyond its normal weight |

## Encumbered

As long as your carried armor and gear isn't more than half your carrying capacity (round down), you can move and act normally. Past this, up to your carrying capacity, you are **encumbered**: your [dash](E9-Combat#dash) rate is halved to 10 paces per round, and you can't [evade](E9-Combat#evade). Being encumbered also makes [Agility](E9-Talents#agility) and [Stealth](E9-Talents#stealth) checks harder.

## Burdened

Past your full carrying capacity, you are **burdened**: your speed is halved (to 3 paces per combat round), your actions are always slow, and you can't dash or evade.
