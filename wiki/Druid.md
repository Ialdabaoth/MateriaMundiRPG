# Druid

*Fey Magic-User*  

### Class Features

#### Prime Ability:

*Wisdom*  

#### Hit Die:

*d6+Con*  

#### Recommended Starting Equipment

Quarterstaff, dagger, light armor, potion belt

#### Martial Training

Druids are not familiar with any nonsimple weapons or armor. Additionally, wearing steel armor or wielding a steel weapon will interfere with their ability to prepare and cast primal magic.

#### Talent Training

At levels 2 and 6, you gain training in **Medicine**; at levels 4 and 8, you gain training in **Survival**.

## Primal Druidcraft

You begin play having learned four first-circle druid glamours or wild shapes. Unlike a wizard, you do not need a spellbook to prepare glamours; once you learn a glamour, you know how to prepare it from memory.

You may learn new glamours from druids, wisps, fair folk, or intelligent fey beasts willing to teach them to you, and may learn new wild shape glamours by communing with the natural animals or fey beasts that you wish to transform into. If your total glamours known exceeds the limit based on your level, you must forget one known glamour or wild shape whenever you learn a new one.

Each sunrise or sunset, you can prepare a number of primal glamours into talismans based on your level, which you can cast during the day. Once a glamour is cast, its talisman becomes un-prepared. You can prepare glamours of a lower circle than indicated.

#### Preparing During Exploration

You can prepare primal glamours during exploration, if you have an empty talisman of a suitable circle and element. This takes a number of Survival checks equal to the chosen glamour’s circle; each roll takes one turn spent preparing the talisman.

#### Wisps

You may also befriend tiny elemental fey spirits, called wisps. By investing preparation into a wisp, you enable it to perform any one elemental glamour on your behalf, up to that preparation's circle.

#### Primal Rituals

You can also perform primal glamours as rituals; each ritual takes one turn per circle. You may perform any glamour that you know as a ritual. Each turn that you perform a ritual requires a Survival check, or the ritual fails. If each roll succeeds, a ritual's effect happens on the next turn after it is completed.

#### Primal Magic and Iron

If you are touching iron or steel, or if you have any iron or steel items on your person, you cannot prepare or cast any primal glamours.
