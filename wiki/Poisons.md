## Poisons

These poisons are usually given to unwilling or unaware targets, either by force-feeding or injection via a specially-designed piercing weapon (treat as a dagger). Force-feeding an unwilling target requires that they first be restrained, while injection requires a successful attack throw that deals at least 1 hp of non-blunt damage. They can also be ingested willingly, if for some reason the poison’s effect is desired.

### Delirium Poison

*Rare Poison*  

*Ingredients- animascaria mushrooms, blue-woad*

*seeds, Wood materia*

Delirium poison causes the imbiber to suffer violent hallucinations and mental confusion. It must roll a d6 at the start of each round to determine its behavior:

1 - The creature uses all its movement to move in a random

direction. Roll a d8 and assign a direction to each die face.

The creature is then stunned for the rest of the round.

2 to 3 - the creature is stunned, and does not move or act.

4 to 5 - the creature uses its action to make a melee attack

against a randomly determined creature within its reach, and

then is stunned for the rest of the round. If there is no

creature within reach, it does nothing this round.

6 - the creature can act and move normally. At the end of the

round, it can save vs. Doom to attempt to end the effect.

### Death Poison

*Any Rarity Poison*  

*Ingredients - Nightshade, Belladonna, Almonds,*

*Wood materia*

One of the most deadly poisons known to man, death poison immediately forces a save, inflicting a critical hit on a failure. On a success, it still inflicts ongoing poison damage. If brewed as a common poison, the saving throw is easy; if brewed as a rare potion, the saving throw is hard.

### Disability Poison

*Uncommon+ Poison*  

*Ingredients - Wood materia, and natural materia*

*that depends on the ability to be poisoned.*

Ability-sapping poison is indistinguishable at first glance from a potion of enhanced ability, but if imbibed, it instead decreases one of the imbiber’s abilities. The imbiber suffers a temporary static penalty to the ability chosen when brewing the potion; this penalty decreases by 1 per exploration turn until it becomes a -1 penalty, which remains until the imbiber sleeps for at least 6 hours.

| Rarity | Penalty |
|---|---|
| Uncommon | -1 |
| Rare | -2 |
| Legendary | -3 |

While you have a penalty to an ability, all task tests linked to that ability are harder.

### Love Potion

*Uncommon Poison*  

*Ingredients - Sassafras root, vanilla extract, rose*

*petals, Wood materia, Illusion or Fey materia*

This brew appears to be a normal elixir of some kind, but when imbibed, its imbiber immediately becomes stunned, wandering about until they find another creature. They immediately lose the stunned condition and become beguiled by the first creature that they see, hear, or touch.

Roll 2d6 x 10 to determine the number of hours that the effect lasts. Whenever the beguiled imbiber could normally deny or snap out of the beguiled condition, they instead subtract one hour from the number of hours remaining on the potion instead of automatically breaking the effect. Once the potion wears off, the imbiber finds all Intuition tests and Wisdom saving throws harder as an aftereffect until they get a full night’s sleep.

### Polymorph Potion

*Rare Poison*  

*Ingredients - a drop of blood, a tooth or bone, and a*

*hair, feather or scale from the animal to transform*

*into, Wood materia*

A Polymorph Potion transforms the imbiber into a specific kind of natural beast for a number of exploration turns equal to its potency. The beast to be transformed into is chosen when the poison is brewed, and some tissue from a creature of that type must be incorporated into the ingredients.

The imbiber must immediately save or be stunned as they readjust to their new shape. If they critically fail, the potion’s effects become permanent.

While transformed, the imbiber’s hit points, features and abilities become those of the species chosen. This includes mental abilities; a transformed creature with an Intelligence of -4 or lower is no longer considered intelligent. The imbiber cannot transform into any other shape while under the effects of the potion, and cannot transform back into their natural shape until the effects wear off.

### Potion of Sleeping Death

*Rare Poison*  

*Ingredients - pufferfish liver, pomegranate seeds,*

*Wood materia*

A potion of sleeping death places its imbiber in a state of suspended animation for a number of hours equal to the poison’s potency. If the brewer knows who the imbiber will be and can examine them thoroughly, and succeeds on a Medicine test when brewing the poison, they may precisely time its duration down to the hour by adjusting the dose. For the duration, the target appears to be dead to all mundane inspection.

Upon waking, the imbiber must  make a hard save or suffer a critical hit.

An unusual and extremely controversial use of this potion is to allow the living to briefly enter the realm of the dead. If the Lay to Rest miracle is performed on someone while under the influence of this poison, the imbiber must save or die; if they succeed, they leave their body as a ghost for the duration of the poison, returning when they awaken.

### Sleeping Powder

*Any Rarity Poison*  

*Ingredients - dream poppy sap, violet hempweed*

*pollen, Wood materia*

The poison is made into a fine powder, which can be blown into an adjacent creature’s face. A creature that breathes in the powder must save or fall unconscious for 2d6 turns.

The imbiber falls into a deep slumber that cannot be shaken until they sleep off the effects. One dose of Common sleeping powder affects a single adjacent creature, while one dose of Uncommon sleeping powder can affect all creatures in a 3-pace cone when blown from the hand, or within a 3-pace diameter sphere when thrown.

If mixed with water and ingested, the powder becomes much more potent, lasting 2d6 hours. Saving vs. an uncommon or rare ingested sleeping potion is hard.

Even if they succeed in their saving throw, a creature poisoned by sleeping powder will be groggy, making all rolls harder for one turn.

### Truth Serum

*Rare Poison*  

*Ingredients - dream poppy sap, rye ergot, magic*

*mushrooms, Illusion or Fey materia*

A creature that imbibes truth serum must make a hard Wit test whenever they attempt to speak. On a failure, they blurt out the most truthful version of whatever they intended to say, regardless of their intent to deceive.

While the effects last, any Wit tests that they make to influence another creature are hard, and Wit tests made by other creatures to get them to divulge information are easy.

Roll 2d6 to determine the number of turns that the effects last. After the effect wears off, the imbiber must make an easy save vs. Doom or suffer a critical hit.
