*Expert class.* Experts focus on skills and tricks.

## Class Features

- **Prime ability:** Dexterity
- **Hit die:** d6+Con
- **Recommended starting equipment:** [Light crossbow](E9-Weapons#light-crossbow), two [daggers](E9-Weapons#dagger), [light armor](E9-Armor-and-Shields#light-armor), lockpicking tools, dungeoneer's kit

## Expertise

Each time you gain a level, including level 1, you gain training in a [talent](E9-Talents) of your choice. Additionally, you may perform feats of uncanny expertise, such as the following.

### Climb Sheer Surface

Climbing a sheer wall is an [Agility](E9-Talents#agility) check rolled at normal difficulty for you, and you automatically succeed at any climbing action that would be at normal difficulty for an untrained creature. You cannot attempt this feat if you are encumbered, wearing medium or heavy armor, or using a shield.

### Pick Lock / Disarm Mechanism

You may make a [Finesse](E9-Talents#finesse) check to attempt to pick a lock or disarm a mechanism that you are aware of. The difficulty depends on the complexity of the mechanism, what tools you have available, and how well you understand it. The difficulties for [picking locks](E9-Exploration#picking-locks) and [disarming traps](E9-Exploration#disarming-traps) assume an expert; anyone else finds the check at least harder, if they can attempt it at all.

### Sneak Attack

You may make a [Stealth](E9-Talents#stealth) check before attacking if the target is not aware of you, rolled at the same difficulty as the attack itself. If you succeed, you skip your d20 attack throw; instead, your attack automatically hits and is a [critical hit](E9-Damage#critical-hits).

## Other Expert Abilities

- Experts can use [stealth](E9-Exploration#stealth) without interfering with other actions.
- An expert can [disarm or pilfer](E9-Combat#disarm-or-pilfer) with a Finesse check after hitting with a grab attack.

## Training Talents

All character classes use talents, but experts especially rely on them. Each time you gain a class level, you pick one of your talents and improve its threshold by 1, to a minimum threshold of 2+. Which talents you used in the last few adventures should have some bearing on which gets trained; if there is no clear focus, training whichever talent has the worst threshold is usually a safe bet.

## Bonus Experience

Experts gain additional experience through recovering treasure. See [Class-Based Experience](E9-Advancement#experts).
