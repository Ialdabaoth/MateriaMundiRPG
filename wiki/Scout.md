# Scout

### ()

*Human Expert*  

### Class Features

#### Prime Ability:

*Dexterity*  

#### Hit Die:

*d6+Con*  

#### Recommended Starting Equipment

Light crossbow, two daggers, light armor, lockpicking tools, dungeoneer’s kit

#### Martial Training

Scouts are familiar with every kind of weapon, but only simple (non-heavy) armor.

#### Talent Training

Each time you gain a level, you gain training in your choice of **Agility**, **Finesse**, **Stealth**, or **Alertness**. At levels 5 and 9, you may instead gain training in any one talent of your choice.

#### Scout's Expertise

Climbing (**Agility**) and hiding (**Stealth**) are particularly easy for you. You automatically succeed at any hiding or climbing action that another class would attempt at a normal difficulty, while tasks that are impossible for other classes, such as climbing a sheer wall or hiding in mere shadows, are rolled at normal difficulty for you. You cannot apply this expertise if you are encumbered, wearing medium or heavy armor, or using a shield.

#### Sneak Attack

You use your ranged attack threshold when you attack a target that is not aware of you, and the attack is a critical hit if each of its damage dice exceeds your Stealth threshold.

## Talents

Your Dexterity, Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma each determine your starting threshold for three different talents; if your ability is +0, then all three of those talents will start at a threshold of 5+. A negative ability adjusts that many talents to a starting threshold of 6+, while a positive ability adjusts that many to 4+.

#### Agility

*(Dexterity) to climb surfaces, maintain*  

balance, and perform precise athletic actions.

#### Finesse

*(Dexterity) to pick locks, disarm traps,*  

operate machinery, or craft or repair items.

#### Stealth

*(Dexterity) to move silently without*  

being noticed, hide from view, surprise or ambush an enemy, or pick pockets.

#### Lore

*(Intelligence) to understand a language,*  

decipher a code, or recall any knowledge involving history, religion, or culture.

#### Medicine

*(Intelligence) to brew an elixir or*  

toxin, or to diagnose or treat injury or disease.

#### Science

*(Intelligence) to brew a potion, enchant*  

or identify a scroll or magic item, notice the presence of supernatural forces, or recall any knowledge involving math or engineering.

#### Alertness

*(Wisdom) to detect a hidden trap or*  

secret door, to listen or watch for enemies, and avoid being surprised by ambushes.

#### Intuition

*(Wisdom) to read the emotions and*  

intentions of living creatures, or to get a "gut sense" of danger or extreme foolishness.

#### Survival

*(Wisdom) to navigate the wilderness,*  

hunt or forage for food, follow others' tracks or hide your own, or set up camp.

#### Bearing

*(Charisma) to maintain composure,*  

manage hirelings and followers, or improve your allies’ morale.

#### Charm

*(Charisma) to sway another intelligent*  

creature’s attitude towards you.

#### Wit

*(Charisma) to influence another intelligent*  

creature’s immediate behavior or beliefs.
