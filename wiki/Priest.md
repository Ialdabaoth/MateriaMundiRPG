# Priest

### ()

*Human Magic-User*  

### Class Features

#### Prime Ability:

*Charisma*  

#### Hit Die:

*d6+Con*  

#### Recommended Starting Equipment

Quarterstaff, medium armor, prayer book, holy symbol, dungeoneer’s kit

#### Martial Training

Priests are not familiar with any non-simple weapons or armor.

#### Talent Training

At levels 2 and 6, you gain training in **Intuition**; at levels 4 and 8, you gain training in **Bearing**.

## Divine Miracles

Each sunrise and sunset, you can prepare a number of divine miracles into prayer strips based on your level, which you can use during the day to perform their associated miracles. Once a miracle is performed, its prayer strip burns to ash.

Choose a domain of faith. You may perform miracles of the associated domain, as well as common miracles, using any prayer strip of the same or higher circle - regardless of what miracle was prepared into it.

#### Preparing During Exploration

You can prepare miracles during exploration, if you have an empty preparation of a suitable circle. This takes a number of Bearing checks equal to the miracle’s circle; each roll takes one turn spent in intense prayer.

#### Rebuking the Unholy

You can flourish a holy symbol and make a Bearing check to attempt to drive back every unholy creature within a 5-pace cone with fewer hit dice than your own. Each affected target functionally becomes panicked for as long as you focus, even if it is normally immune to fear-based effects. If you are fifth level or higher, any affected targets with only one hit die are destroyed or banished, instead of driven back. At ninth level, affected targets with two hit dice are destroyed as well.

#### Divine Rituals

You can also perform miracles from your prayerbook as divine rituals; each ritual takes one turn per circle. You may perform any miracle up to a circle of half your level (round up) as a ritual. Each round that you perform a ritual requires a Bearing check, or the ritual fails. If each roll succeeds, a ritual's effect happens on the next turn after it is completed.

#### Sworn Oaths

Divine blessings can bestow semi-permanent effects on anyone who swears and upholds a particular oath as the ritual is performed. Any blessing requiring focus to maintain can be maintained instead via sworn oath.Violating that oath will immediately reverse the effects of the blessing into a curse that can only be removed once you have atoned.
