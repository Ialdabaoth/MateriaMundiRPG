## Fey Monsters

The unbridled creativity and chaos of Faerie alone means that no bestiary can ever be complete; strange new beasts are spawned every day in the deep wilds of the Fey, some of whom will inevitably manage to survive in the Material world. A fey monster is similar to a fey beast, but its mind and body are too alien to be targeted with glamours such as call beasts or beast bond. As such, fey monsters can never be learned as wild shape forms.

### Bloodstirge

*(Level 1)*  

Bloodstirges are giant, bloated mosquitoes with powerful gripping claws on their forelegs. While not particularly dangerous alone, a large swarm of bloodstirges can drain a mortal of blood in seconds.
```
Doom 17+ Hold 17+ Blast 11+ Trick 18+ Curse 14+ Fear  —
Movement:      Fly             Size:  Tiny
Armor:         0               HP:    1
Attacks (17+): latch on (grapple), bite (1 hp)
Con -3 Str -3  Dex +3 Int -4   Wis +0 Cha  —
Talents (5+):  Agility, Stealth, Alertness
Senses:        Can sense warm-blooded creatures
               from up to 10 paces away
```
**Drain Blood -**  A stirge that latches onto a victim will stay attached for 1d6 combat rounds. Each round, the victim must save vs. Doom or suffer -1 to Constitution (reducing both hit points and maximum hit points accordingly).

Lost Constitution is healed via bedrest before any hit point damage is regained naturally. It can also be healed with a restoration miracle. **Disease Carrier -** A creature bitten by a stirge that achieves a critical hit, or a creature with a stirge latched onto it when that stirge is killed, must make a saving throw vs. Doom or contract a nasty bloodborne infection.

### Rustgrub

*(Level 2)*  

Rustgrubs are the larvae of rust hulks, which are capable of chewing through and digesting metal. They are attracted to iron and steel but ignore flesh.

*Harvesting - If properly harvested, a rustgrub*

*yields 2d6 materia charges, half of which are water*

*materia and half of which are stone materia.*
```
Doom 11+ Hold 16+ Blast 15+ Trick 17+ Curse 15+ Fear  —
Movement:      Slow           Size:   Medium
Armor:         1              HP:     (2d6+4)
Attacks (17+): bite (0 hp, sundering)
Con +2 Str -3  Dex -2  Int -4 Wis -2  Cha   —
Talents (4+):  Stealth
Senses:        Can sense ferrous metals up to 5 paces
away
```
**Rust Metal -** A rustgrub’s saliva will reduce the effectiveness of any steel weapon, shield, or armor by 1 point per round until it is either washed off, or the gear is completely destroyed. Any non-metallic gear with steel or iron fasteners instead makes a 12+ saving throw each round that it is exposed.

**Spit -** A rustgrub has two glamour, which it can spend to propel a gob of corrosive saliva at anyone within 3 paces that it senses is wearing steel armor or wielding a steel weapon (treat as a range 1 thrown attack).

This deals zero hit point damage, but begins sundering steel weapons at 1 point per round until it is washed off, or the gear is completely destroyed.

### Fire Beetle

*(Level 3)*  

Fire beetles are massive beetles the size of a large dog, with brightly glowing patches on the sides of their head and abdomen. When threatened, they can produce a corrosive and highly flammable substance, which they then ignite and spray at the source of the threat.

*Harvesting - If properly harvested, a fire beetle*

*yields 3d6 charges of flame materia.*
```
Doom 10+ Hold 12+ Blast 12+ Trick 16+ Curse 12+ Fear  —
Movement:      Slow, Fly Slow  Size:  Small
Armor:         3               HP:    (3d6+6)÷2
Attacks (12+): bite (1d6 hp)
Con +2 Str +0  Dex +0 Int -4   Wis +0 Cha   —
Talents (4+):  None
```
**Bioluminescence -** A fire beetle’s lightspots produce bright light out to 2 paces, and dim light out to 5 paces.

**Flamethrower -** A fire beetle has three points of glamour, which it can use to generate a 4pace cone of flame in front of it that inflicts 2d6 fire damage.

### Cave Spider

*(Level 4)*  

Cave Spiders are overgrown spiders infused with energy from Faerie. While some cave spiders foray aboveground, most make their homes in dark caverns or abandoned ruins, where they can spin elaborate webs to trap prey. Cave spider silk is an important ingredient in various alchemical potions and magic items.

*Harvesting - A cave spider’s web can be harvested*

*for 4d6 dinar worth of spider silk.*
```
Doom 9+ Hold 10+ Blast 9+ Trick 15+ Curse 10+ Fear  —
Movement:      Climb          Size:   Large
Armor:         1              HP:     (4d6+8)x2
Attacks (10+): ensnare (grapple), bite (2d6 poison)
Con +2 Str +1  Dex +2 Int -4  Wis +1  Cha   —
Talents (4+):  Agility, Stealth, Alertness
```
**Ensnaring Web -** A creature grappled by the spider is restrained and immediately wrapped in webbing, immobilizing the victim until it saves to break free.

**Poison Bite -**  A cave spider’s bite inflicts ongoing poison damage, making the victim's actions slower until they make a successful save vs. Doom.

**Web Tremors -** Cave spiders will lay almost invisibly thin strands of webbing across a large area surrounding their lair that conducts vibrations back to the spider, allowing it to sense the presence of any creature within 5’ of its web. Vibrations as subtle as breathing or a heartbeat can be picked up by the web. A cave spider’s webbing will typically stretch to cover an area up to 100’ from its lair.

### Gelatinous Ooze

*(Level 5)*  

Gelatinous Oozes are giant slime-mold colonies infused with water materia, making them nearly transparent and enhancing their acidic mucus to supernatural levels. An ooze is a mindless creature, which simply moves forward inexorably as it engulfs and digests any organic matter that it comes across - leaving inorganic material embedded in its bulk.  While in a corridor less than 3 paces wide, the ooze’s shape perfectly fills the entire corridor to a depth and height of 10 feet.

*Harvesting - An ooze’s gelatinous remains can be*

*alchemically processed to render 5d6 charges of*

*water materia.*
```
Doom 5+ Hold 6+ Blast 12+ Trick  — Curse  — Fear  —
Movement:      1 pace          Size:  Large
Armor:         3 (vulnerable cold) HP: 5d6+25
Attacks (6+):  engulf (grapple), dissolve (2d6 acid)
Con +5 Str +4  Dex -2  Int  —  Wis  — Cha  —
Talents (2+):  Stealth
```
**Paralyzing Mucus -** Any creature grappled by the ooze suffers acid damage from a dissolve attack at the end of the round, and must also save vs. Hold to not be rendered helpless.

Any creature that succeeds is hobbled and its actions are slower, but it may attempt to escape the ooze’s grapple on its next action with another hard save vs. Hold.

**Digestion -** Any acid damage dealt to a living creature by the ooze also heals the ooze for that many hit points. If this increases the ooze above its current maximum hit points, it immediately increases in size until it can accommodate its new hit point total.

**Mitosis -** A large ooze that is reduced to below half its hit points by weapon damage splits into two medium-sized oozes, each with half the original creature’s starting hit points; a medium ooze splits into two small oozes, and a small ooze splits into two tiny oozes.

### Rust Hulk

*(Level 6)*  

Rust hulks appear to be a cross between a giant beetle and a gorilla, with a dark brown shell made of rusted iron.

*Harvesting -*  *If properly harvested, a rust hulk*

*yields 6d6 charges of stone materia.*
```
Doom 5+ Hold 5+ Blast 10+ Trick 11+ Curse 9+ Fear 9+
Movement:      Slow, Fly Slow Size:   Large
Armor:         3              HP:     6d6 + 5 SP
Attacks (5+):  claws (2d6 blunt), mandibles (1d6 piercing)
Con +4 Str +4  Dex -1  Int -2 Wis +0 Cha +0
Talents:       None
```
**Hard Shell -** A rust hulk’s structure points must be depleted before any hit point damage may be dealt, unless the attacker achieves a critical hit.

**Frenzy -** When any hit point damage is inflicted on a rust hulk, it may make two melee attacks on its next action.

### Mimic

*(Level 7)*  

Mimics are a strange kind of air-breathing cephalopod, which can adjust its coloration and shape to match nearly any small or medium object. Mimics are extraordinarily clever animals; a mimic in a dungeon will often transform itself into the appearance of valuable treasure, in order to lure unwary adventurers close. Once its prey is close enough, it lashes out with six long, rope-like tentacles to draw the victim towards its beak.

*Harvesting - If properly harvested, a mimic yields*

*7d6 charges of fey or dream materia.*
```
Doom 8+ Hold 8+ Blast 5+ Trick 9+ Curse 8+ Fear 8+
Movement:      Normal          Size:  Small
Armor:         0               HP:    7d6÷2
Attacks (9+):  grapple (reach 2), bite (2d6 piercing)
Con +0 Str +0  Dex +3 Int -1   Wis +0 Cha +0
Talents (3+):  Agility, Finesse, Stealth
```
**Crushing Hug -** A mimic’s tentacles begin squeezing once they grapple a target, dealing 1d6 bashing damage per round. Biting any target that it has already grappled is easier. **Poison Bite -** Any creature bitten by a mimic is poisoned, slowing its actions until it makes a successful save vs. Doom.

**Camouflage -** Detecting a camouflaged mimic is a hard Alertness check.  Mimics prefer to attack from ambush with their grappling tentacles; a hidden mimic can sneak attack like a Scout or Hunter. Any successful sneak attack also hobbles its target.

### Roper & Piercers

*(Level 8)*  

A Roper is a mollusc that evolved to metabolize Earth materia, allowing it to grow to enormous size and camouflage itself as a stalagmite. Its young are called piercers, and hang from the ceiling near their parent, waiting to drop on unsuspecting creatures and impale them with their sharp shells.

*Harvesting - If properly harvested, a roper’s shell*

*yields 8d6 charges of stone materia.*
```
Doom 5+ Hold 5+ Blast 5+ Trick 9+ Curse 7+ Fear 7+
Movement:      climb 3 paces  Size:   Large
Armor:         3              HP:     8d6 + 6 SP
Attacks (5+ x2): 4 tentacles (grapple), bite (1d6 piercing)
Con +4 Str +2  Dex +2 Int -2  Wis +0 Cha +0
Talents (4+):  Agility, Finesse, Stealth
```
**Hard Shell -** A roper’s structure points must be depleted before any hit point damage may be dealt, unless the attacker achieves a critical hit. Its shell is indistinguishable from a stalagmite when not moving.

**Paralyzing Bite -** The roper’s bite injects a paralyzing poison, slowing the victim's actions until it successfully saves vs. Doom. An affected  creature is also hobbled until they save.

Piercer Nest - The roper will be accompanied by 1d6+1

piercers (small 1 hd larval ropers with  2 hp and 1 sp each),

which cling to the ceiling and resemble stalactites. Piercers

drop down on creatures grappled by the roper; treat each

dropping piercer as a sneak attack with an attack threshold of

12+ that deals 1d6 piercing damage and achieves a critical hit if

the damage die rolls a '6'. Piercers that critically hit embed

themselves into the target and must be dug out, or they will

keep their host paralyzed as they slowly devour them from the

inside. Digging out a piercer requires a hard Medicine check

while inflicting damage onto the victim with a dagger.
