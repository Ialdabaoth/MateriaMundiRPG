## Damage

Damage that gets through your [armor](E9-Armor-and-Shields) reduces your hit points. If your hit points are reduced below half your maximum, you are [injured](E9-Conditions#injured); if they are reduced to zero, you are [dying](E9-Conditions#dying).

Whenever you inflict damage with an attack, if any of the damage dice roll a 6, an additional effect might be inflicted on the target. This effect depends on the type of damage inflicted.

## Weapon Damage

### Blunt Damage

Physical attacks with the [blunt](E9-Weapons#blunt) property stagger whenever a 6 is rolled, forcing the affected target to make a save vs. [Hold](E9-Abilities#hold) to avoid being knocked down or pushed back (attacker's choice) by the force of the blow. This saving throw is easy if only one 6 was rolled in the damage roll, or hard if two or more 6s were rolled.

### Entangling Damage

Attacks made with the [entangling](E9-Weapons#entangling) property have a similar effect, knocking down or disarming the target (attacker's choice) if they fail their save vs. Hold. However, any Doom saving throw rolled to resist a critical hit made by a blunt or entangling weapon is easier.

### Sundering Damage

Attacks with the [sundering](E9-Weapons#sundering) property reduce the target's armor by 1 per 6 rolled until repaired. If the target has a shield, any 6 rolled destroys the shield instead. However, it is easier to hit you with an interrupt attack while you are attacking with a sundering weapon.

## Elemental Damage

Elemental damage effects depend on the element. Many elemental attacks, such as **acid**, **poison**, or **fire**, can inflict ongoing damage. Many others, such as **psychic**, **cold**, **thunder**, **lightning**, or **radiant**, might [stun](E9-Conditions#stunned) the target. Thunder and radiant damage can also affect the target's senses, deafening or [blinding](E9-Conditions#blind) them for a round. Cold or petrification might [hobble](E9-Conditions#hobbled) or restrain the target until they break free, and acid can sunder armor or weapons. Psychic damage might frighten or confuse the target for a round.

## Critical Hits

If an attack throw rolls a 20, it's a critical hit. [Experts](E9-Expert#sneak-attack) can also sneak attack to cause a critical hit, and [fighters](E9-Fighter#fighter-training) achieve a critical hit whenever their attack roll beats its threshold by 10 or more (once their threshold is 9+ or better).

When a creature suffers a critical hit, it immediately saves vs. [Doom](E9-Abilities#doom) after the main attack damage is applied. If it successfully saves, it loses half its current hit points (round the loss down, so a successful save will never drop a creature to zero hit points); if it fails, its hit points drop to zero and it is [dying](E9-Conditions#dying).
