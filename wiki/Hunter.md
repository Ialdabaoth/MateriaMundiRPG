# Hunter

## ()

*Fey Expert*  

### Class Features

#### Prime Ability:

*Dexterity*  

#### Hit Die:

*d6+Con*  

#### Recommended Starting Equipment

Shortbow, two daggers, light armor

#### Martial Training

Hunters are familiar with every kind of weapon, but only simple (non-heavy) armor.

#### Talent Training

Each time you gain a level, you gain training in your choice of **Agility**, **Stealth**, **Alertness**, or **Survival**. At levels 5 and 9, you may instead gain training in any one talent of your choice.

#### Hunter's Expertise

Tracking and navigating (**Survival**) are particularly easy for you. You automatically succeed at any tracking or navigation task that another class could attempt at a normal difficulty, while tasks that are impossible for other classes, such as retracing the actions and condition of a creature merely by studying its tracks, navigating merely by the patterns of the local terrain and wildlife, or befriending and training an undomesticated wild animal, are rolled at normal difficulty for you.

#### Sneak Attack

You use your ranged attack threshold when you attack a target that is not aware of you, and the attack is a critical hit if each of its damage dice exceeds your Stealth threshold.
