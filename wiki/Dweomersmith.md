# Dweomersmith()

*Dwarf Magic-User*  

### Class Features

#### Prime Ability:

*Intelligence*  

#### Hit Die:

*d6+Con+1*  

#### Recommended Starting Equipment

Hammer, crossbow, heavy armor, potion belt, smith’s tools, artisan’s tools, dungeoneer’s kit

#### Martial Training

Dweomersmiths are familiar with every kind of weapon, armor, and shield.

#### Talent Training

At levels 2 and 6, you gain training in **Finesse**; at levels 4 and 8, you gain training in **Science**.

## Dweomercraft

You begin play with a craftbook, which holds two common alchemical formulae and two common magic item blueprints. As you find other formulae or blueprints in the dungeon, you may copy them into your own craftbook.

Each sunrise or sunset, you can prepare dweomers from your craftbook, which are volatile alchemical formulae or magic item enchantments. You can choose to prepare dweomers of a lower rarity than indicated.

#### Preparing During Exploration

You can prepare alchemy dweomers during exploration, if you have fewer than your maximum number of dweomers prepared; or you may use this feat to recharge a dweomer magic item of the appropriate rarity that has expended its charge. This takes a number of successful Science checks equal to the item or dweomer’s rarity; each roll is one turn spent studying your craftbook while you enchant the dweomer.

#### Dweomersmith Rituals

You can also perform certain dweomers from your craftbook as rituals; each first-circle ritual takes one turn. At third level, you can also perform second-circle rituals, each of which takes two turns; at fifth level, you can perform third-circle rituals, which take three turns. Each round that you perform a ritual requires a Science check, or the ritual fails. If each roll succeeds, a ritual's effect happens on the next turn after it is completed.
