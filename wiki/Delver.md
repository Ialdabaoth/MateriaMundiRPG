# Delver

### ()

*Dwarf Expert*  

### Class Features

#### Prime Ability:

*Wisdom*  

#### Hit Die:

*d6+Con+1*  

#### Recommended Starting Equipment

Pickaxe, light crossbow, medium armor, potion belt, dungeoneer’s kit

#### Martial Training

Delvers are familiar with every kind of weapon, armor, and shield.

#### Talent Training

Each time you gain a level, you gain training in your choice of **Finesse**, **Science**, **Alertness,** or **Survival**. At levels 5 and 9, you may instead gain training in any one talent of your choice.

#### Armored Stealth

Your **Stealth** checks are not penalized when you wear medium armor which has been properly fitted and prepared to not reflect light or make noise. You can prepare armor with a **Finesse** check as an exploration action.

#### Stonesense

When in a natural cave, or any other structure composed primarily of stone, you may spend one turn tapping the walls with a hard object and listening carefully, then make a **Survival** check to map out the area for 5 paces in all directions. You can even detect passageways on the other side of solid walls. If there are any mechanisms built into the walls of this area, a hard **Alertness** check will detect them all.
