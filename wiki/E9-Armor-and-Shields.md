## Armor

Armor reduces the damage from each weapon by its **defense** value, to a minimum of zero damage per weapon. Most armors offer no protection from elemental or magical attacks.

| Armor type | Defense | Weight | Other properties |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Light](#light-armor) | 1 | 1 | |
| [Medium](#medium-armor) | 2 | 2 | Steel, interferes with Stealth |
| [Heavy](#heavy-armor) | 3 | 3 | Steel, interferes with Agility and Stealth |

Weapons with the [piercing](E9-Weapons#piercing) property ignore light or medium armor when inflicting their damage. Armor's weight for [encumbrance](E9-Encumbrance) is equal to its defense.

### Light Armor

Defense 1, weight 1. Wizards in light armor maintain one fewer first-circle and one fewer second-circle preparation (see [Arcane Magic and Armor](E9-Wizard#arcane-magic-and-armor)).

### Medium Armor

Defense 2, weight 2. Steel. Makes [Stealth](E9-Talents#stealth) checks harder. Wizards cannot cast prepared spells in medium armor.

### Heavy Armor

Defense 3, weight 3. Steel. Makes [Agility](E9-Talents#agility) and [Stealth](E9-Talents#stealth) checks harder. Wizards cannot cast prepared spells in heavy armor.

## Shields

### Shield

In addition to armor, some character classes can use shields. A shield is wielded in your off hand, preventing you from wielding weapons with that hand. In exchange, you are **harder to hit** with any attack that you are aware of, as long as you are capable of moving your shield to block it.

A shield counts as one point of weight for [encumbrance](E9-Encumbrance). Using a shield makes Agility and Stealth checks harder, prevents an expert from [climbing sheer surfaces](E9-Expert#climb-sheer-surface), and stops a wizard from casting prepared spells. A [sundering](E9-Weapons#sundering) weapon destroys a shield on any 6 rolled for damage.
