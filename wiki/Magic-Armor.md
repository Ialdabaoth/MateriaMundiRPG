## Magic Armor

Enchanted armor typically adds its rarity level to its Armor rating, including vs. piercing effects, to a maximum Armor rating of 5.

Some enchanted shields can be used to block area effects or effects that don't use an attack throw, making your saving throw easier.

### Bracer of Shielding

*Any Rarity (Arcane)*  

*Ingredients: Aether Pearl, feysilver*

When deactivated, a bracer of shielding looks like a fine bluish-silver bracer inlaid with ornate runes and a clear stone against the back of the wrist. Activating it causes the stone to glow softly in the dark, pulsing brighter when touched or tapped with any force.

A bracer of shielding acts as a shield that can defend against ranged, elemental, and aetheric attacks as well as physical melee attacks, without any weight and without occupying the wearer's shield hand. The bracer also subtracts twice its rarity level from any aetheric damage that the wearer receives. The shield only becomes visible and solid when blocking a potentially damaging shot, appearing as a glowing, translucent disc made of crackling geometric hexagons with a brightness based on the force of the impact.

### Dwarven Apron

*Common (Arcane)*  

*Ingredients: Flame Pearl, orichalc, firebat leather*

A dwarven apron appears to be a thick padded leather suit and blacksmith’s apron.

Upon closer inspection, thick plates of a fibrous black mineral are woven into the leather, and dark gold filigree patterns are woven along its edge.

As +1 light armor, a dwarven apron grants a Defense of 2. It additionally protects from heat and cold, and reduces all fire damage that the wearer receives as if it was weapon damage.

### Feysilver Armor

*Rare (Arcane or Primal)*  

*Ingredients: feysilver*

When used for armor, feysilver appears as a shimmering white metal with a mirror finish. Feysilver can be used to make any masterwork armor or shield that would normally be made from steel. Any feysilver armor counts as one category lighter, both for purposes of stealth and encumbrance - feysilver chainmail counts as light armor while providing the protection of masterwork medium armor (3), while feysilver plate counts as medium armor while providing the protection of masterwork heavy armor (4). Additionally, armor made from feysilver doesn't interfere with primal magics the way iron or steel do.

Feysilver will need to be hidden under clothing to be stealthy. A feysilver shield lowers its encumbrance by one, but still impedes stealth due to its bulk.

### Flying Shield

*Rare (Arcane or Primal)*  

*Ingredients: feysilver, pegasus feathers, Air Pearl*

A flying shield appears to be a fine bluishsilver medium round shield mounted on a bracer of similar metal. When activated, the shield detaches from the bracer and hovers in mid-air, protecting the wearer while leaving their shield arm free to hold a weapon or item. The shield floats back into place and reattaches to the bracer after 1d6 minutes. Once activated, this effect cannot be used again for one hour.

### Living Armor

*Uncommon (Primal)*  

*Ingredients: ironwood, jade, strangler vine, Wood*

*Pearl, Earth Pearl, Water Pearl*

Living armor is a suit of leather armor made from interlocking segments of living oak and green jade stone, woven together with strips of strangler vine. As +1 light armor, it grants a Defense of 2.

Living armor’s most unique feature is its ability to self-repair. If living armor is sundered, it can be planted in fertile soil, where it will slowly knit itself back together at the rate of 1 point of lost Defense per day.

### Mage Robes

*Any Rarity (Arcane)*  

*Ingredients: duskworm silk, feysilver, Aether Pearl*

Mage robes are finely-woven silk robes with a gemstone clasp, and metallic threads woven throughout in arcane patterns. The robes are enchanted to grant a Defense equal to their rarity without interfering with a wizard’s spellcasting ability, so a wizard can wear them as if unarmored.

The robes’ enchantment also adds its bonus to any saving throw to maintain focus or to resist any elemental or magical attack, or to any saving throw or task check made as part of casting an arcane spell.

### Mirror Shield

*Rare (Arcane)*  

*Ingredients: feysilver, Aether Pearl*

A mirror shield appears to be a large shield with a mirror-like finish on its outer surface, and an inlaid opalescent gem on its wrist strap. The shield can be used once per round with a successful Finesse check to block any attack, including a ranged or magical attack, and can even block a blast or cone.

If you successfully block a magic ranged or area attack, you may activate the shield to reflect that attack back onto the attacker, or onto another creature or space of the shieldbearer’s choice within 5 paces of the shieldbearer. The shield-bearer makes a new ranged attack throw for the reflected attack. Once this power is activated, it cannot be used again until the next sunrise or sunset.

### Orichalc Plate

*Uncommon (Arcane)*  

*Ingredients: orichalc, Earth Pearl*

Orichalc plate appears to be a finely wrought suit of heavy armor made of a bronze metal. It grants a total Defense of 5, but is noticeably heavier than an equivalent suit made from steel, adding +1 to its encumbrance (for a total encumbrance of 4). When also wearing the suit’s helm (which has an Earth Pearl inset above the visor's forehead), all saves vs.

critical hits from weapon attacks are easier.

### Robes of Disguise

*Rarer (Arcane)*  

*Ingredients: duskworm silk, feysilver, Dream Pearl*

Robes of disguise are finely-woven grey linen robes with dark silver metallic threads woven throughout in hypnotic spirals. As cloth, robes of disguise do not grant any innate Defense. The wearer of these robes may activate them to change their appearance and voice to match another person that they have interacted with in person for at least an hour with roughly the same size and body shape as the observer (no more than 25% larger or smaller).

As an illusion effect, an observer that inspects the wearer's appearance closely may make a hard save vs. Trick to notice the illusion; this save is easy if the inspector also touches the wearer while inspecting them.

### Robes of Splendour

*Any Rarity (Arcane)*  

*Ingredients: duskworm silk, orichalc, Dream Pearl*

Robes of splendour are expensive, finelywoven silk robes with metallic threads woven throughout in ornamental patterns. Upon closer inspection, arcane sigils and runes are incorporated into the robe’s ornamentation at key points. As cloth, robes of splendour do not grant any innate Defense.

The wearer of these robes gains a bonus to their d6 Charm checks. This bonus is equal to the robes’ rarity: +1 rank for Common robes, +2 for Uncommon robes, or +3 for Rare robes. If another magical effect also increases the wearer’s Charm check rolls, only the highest effect takes hold.

### Thornweave Hide

*Any Rarity (Primal)*  

*Ingredients: owlbear pelt, lasher thorns, Wood*

*Pearl*

A Thornweave Hide appears to be a bearskin pelt with fine owl feather trim and a clasp made from a bright golden-green gem. On closer inspection, thick wooden spines are embedded deep in the fur. As +1 light armor, it grants a Defense of 2.

When hit by a melee attack, the armor's thorns retaliate against the attacker, automatically inflicting piercing damage equal to twice the armor's rarity bonus.
