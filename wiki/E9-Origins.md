Every character has an origin, representing their cultural and ethnic background. This is much broader than our world's ethnicities: this is a world with dwarves, elves, and even stranger creatures! Some origins even have a few abilities that tend to be higher or lower, on average, than a human's.

Your origin determines which character [[Classes|E9-Classes]] you can take. Each origin always has at least one fighter class, one expert class, and one magic-user class to choose from. Within those choices, your [[Abilities|E9-Abilities]] determine which classes you would be better or worse at.

## Rolling an Origin

Start creating a new character by rolling a die to determine your origin. The referee will specify the chart to roll on, and will fill that chart with demographics appropriate to their world. Most rolls should result in a "human" origin, with no ability adjustments. Other origins within the referee's chosen setting might have a single enhanced ability (marked **[+]**), or two enhanced abilities and one deficient ability (marked **[−]**).

## Origin Table

| d6 | Origin | Height | Adjusted abilities |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1-3 | [Human](#human) | 4d6+54 in | none |
| 4 | [Elf](#elf) | 3d6+50 in | +Dex, +Wis, −Con |
| 5 | [Dwarf](#dwarf) | 2d6+40 in | +Con, +Str, −Dex |
| 6 | [Gnome](#gnome) | 1d6+30 in | +Dex, +Cha, −Str |

The example chart represents population demographics typical for a medieval fantasy world; the referee is encouraged to adjust it to reflect the nature of their campaign setting. For example, a campaign set in a wilder area might instead roll a d20 for origin, with some origins even having variations chosen by a second d6 or d20 roll.

### Human

Height 4d6+54 inches. No enhanced or deficient abilities. As an option, the referee might allow anyone playing a human character to choose and swap a single pair of abilities after rolling.

### Elf

Height 3d6+50 inches. Enhanced Dexterity and Wisdom; deficient Constitution.

### Dwarf

Height 2d6+40 inches. Enhanced Constitution and Strength; deficient Dexterity. Due to their small size, dwarves can't wield heavy weapons, and must wield medium weapons two-handed (see [[Weapons|E9-Weapons]]).

### Gnome

Height 1d6+30 inches. Enhanced Dexterity and Charisma; deficient Strength. Due to their small size, gnomes can't wield heavy weapons, and must wield medium weapons two-handed (see [[Weapons|E9-Weapons]]).

## Enhanced and Deficient Abilities

Roll 3d6 in order for each ability, or roll 4d6 for any of your origin's adjusted abilities: for an enhanced **[+]** ability drop the lowest die, and for a deficient **[−]** ability drop the highest die. Add the remaining three dice together and consult the [ability table](E9-Abilities#ability-table).
