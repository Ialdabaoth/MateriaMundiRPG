## Rings

Any magic item which is worn on the hand without needing to be held is considered a ring.

### Aetheric Glove

*Rare (Arcane)*  

*Ingredients: Aether Pearl, feysilver, giant spider silk*

An aetheric glove resembles an expensive silk or leather glove with arcane runes and sigils stitched into it in metallic threads. When worn, the glove grants the wearer at-will use of the mage hand cantrip as long as it is charged.

As an action, the wearer can make a particular gesture with their hand to cast the forceful hand spell. Once used, the glove recharges after the next sunrise or sunset.

### Golem Prosthesis

*Uncommon+ (Arcane)*  

*Ingredients: ironwood, devil creeper, jade,*

*hippogriff leather, Wood Pearl*

A golem prosthesis is an artificial arm, designed to replace a maimed or amputated limb. A single replaced arm or hand is an Uncommon item, and operates nearly identically to the wearer’s original limb, while also granting a +1 to Armor when not wearing medium or heavy armor or a shield, and allowing you to parry melee attacks. A rare prosthesis may incorporate another common or uncommon ring enchantment into its design, while a legendary prosthesis may incorporate a rare enchantment.

### Grip Gloves

*Rare (Arcane or Primal)*  

*Ingredients: giant gecko hide, orichalc, Earth Pearl*

Grip gloves appear to be a pair of lizard-skin gloves with ridged palms. Closer inspection will reveal small metallic runes woven within the sole ridges. While wearing grip gloves, you can use your hands to climb any surface, even upside-down, as if under the effects of the spider climb spell. Wearing grip gloves and grip boots together make rolls to maintain your grip against being dislodged from a surface automatically succeed.

### Rings of Companionship

*Rare (Arcane or Primal)*  

*Ingredients: Wood Pearl, Aetheric materia,*

*feysilver, orichalc, silver, gold*

Rings of Companionship are paired rings, one silver and one gold. When worn by two different creatures who are not hostile to each other, the wearers can communicate telepathically across any distance.

Additionally, while they are within 20 paces of each other, all damage that either would take is split evenly between both of them, and each may help the other as a free action on any check or saving throw that the referee deems appropriate.

### Ring of Disguise

*Any Rarity (Arcane)*  

*Ingredients: Dream Pearl, feysilver, silver*

A ring of disguise appears to be a finely made silver ring with a polished opal gem. On closer inspection, the inside of the ring contains tiny sigils and runes, filled with a metal of a slightly different color.

As their action, the wearer may transform their appearance into any similarly sized and shaped creature, casting the disguise self spell on the wearer. Roll 1d6 per rarity level; the effect lasts for a number of exploration turns equal to the result. Once used, a common ring cannot be used again until after the next sunrise or sunset, while an uncommon ring requires an hour to recharge, and a rare ring requires ten minutes to recharge.

### Ring of Elemental Power

*Any Rarity (Arcane or Primal)*  

*Ingredients: Dragon Pearl, orichalc, gold or silver*

A ring of elemental power appears to be a finely made gold or silver ring with a large, brightly colored gemstone. On closer inspection, the inside of the ring contains tiny sigils and runes, filled with metal of a slightly different color. The ring functions identically to a dragon pearl of the same rarity, but the wearer can hold other items in their hand while using it.

### Ring of Guidance

*Uncommon (Arcane or Primal)*  

*Ingredients: Earth Pearl, orichalc, gold*

A ring of guidance appears to be a heavy gold ring with a polished dark gem. On closer inspection, the inside of the ring contains tiny sigils and runes, filled with a metal of a slightly different color.

To activate the gem, the wearer must kiss it while thinking of an object, creature, or place that is personally important to them. Once activated, a small six-pointed star of light appears within the gem, which points in the direction towards the desired target. This effect lasts until the next sunrise or sunset, at which point the ring cannot be activated again until the next sunrise or sunset.

If the target no longer exists, or is not on the same plane of existence as the wearer, the ring will flash brightly, and then the gemstone will crack. Repairing the physical damage will not restore the ring’s magic without a new earth pearl gemstone.

### Ring of Protection

*Uncommon (Arcane)*  

*Ingredients: Aether Pearl, feysilver, silver*

A ring of protection appears to be a finely made silver ring with a polished clear gem.

On closer inspection, the inside of the ring contains tiny sigils and runes, filled with a metal of a slightly different color. If the wearer would be injured by any physical weapon or aetheric damage attack, the ring immediately casts the shield spell. This spell lasts 1d6 rounds without requiring the wearer’s focus to maintain. Once the ring has been activated, it can’t activate again until it has spent an hour recharging.

If a creature is wearing two rings of protection, or a ring of protection and an amulet of protection, all will activate together whenever their activation conditions are met, but only the highest die result will take effect.

### Ring of Regeneration

*Legendary (Arcane or Primal)*  

*Ingredients: Wood Pearl, orichalc, gold, phoenix*

*down feathers, giant axolotl blood*

A ring of regeneration appears to be a finely made gold ring with a polished jade gem. On closer inspection, the inside of the ring contains tiny sigils and runes, filled with a metal of a slightly different color.

The ring contains incredibly powerful healing magic. While worn, you automatically recover 1 hp at the end of each round of combat, and all saving throws vs. Doom that you make are easy. Additionally, whenever you use any healing ability that requires touching the injured character, any dice rolled to determine hit points recovered are considered to have rolled their maximum possible value.

You automatically roll one hit die during each exploration turn. Once all hp damage has been healed, you make a saving throw vs.

Doom to heal one lasting injury that has not yet become permanent. During each night of sleep, you automatically heal all hp damage and one lasting injury, starting with those injuries that have not yet become permanent.

### Ring of Warding

*Rare (Arcane)*  

*Ingredients: Aether Pearl, orichalc, gold, silver*

A ring of warding appears to be a finely made gold and silver ring with a diamond gem. On closer inspection, the outside and inside of the ring contains tiny sigils and runes, filled with metals of a slightly different color.

The wearer may use a gesture to cast dispel magic from the ring. Once used, it may not be used again until the next sunrise or sunset.
