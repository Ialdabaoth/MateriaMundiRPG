Your character has six primary abilities, which define how tough, strong, nimble, smart, perceptive, and outgoing they are compared to the average person. Each ability ranges from **−3** (severely disabled) to **+3** (world-class prodigy), with most humans typically having abilities of +0. An ability of −1 or lower is noticeably worse than the average human's, while an ability of +1 or higher is noticeably better.

## Rolling Abilities

When you create a character, you roll 3d6 and add the dice together for each ability, generating a number from 3 to 18 which then converts into a −3 to +3 ability value. Certain [[Origins|E9-Origins]] provide enhanced or deficient abilities; you roll 4d6 for those abilities, then ignore either the highest or lowest die and add the rest together.

You always roll your abilities in order (Constitution, Strength, Dexterity, Intelligence, Wisdom, Charisma). This might result in a character with exceptionally high Intelligence but abysmally low Wisdom, but such is the way of things sometimes. As an option, the referee might allow anyone playing a human character to choose and swap a single pair of abilities after rolling.

Barring serious injury or miraculous blessing, these numbers will be your character's innate abilities throughout your career. You can increase your skill at anything with sufficient training and effort, but raw inborn ability will always be what it is.

> **My abilities suck! What do I do?**
> Sometimes the dice just aren't your friend. If you roll a character that seems utterly unsuited to adventuring, the referee might tell you to start over and roll all your abilities again. Don't expect this, however, and don't feel too worried if your abilities are sub-par. As long as you have at least one exceptional ability (+1 or better), you should try to stick with it and see how your character can learn to overcome whatever disabilities the dice might have saddled them with at the beginning of their career. Also, keep in mind that this is a cooperative game: the other members of your party should have your back in situations when you are disadvantaged, and you should have theirs when the tables are turned. You're all in this dungeon together!

Each ability adjusts any saving throws, attack throws, weapon damage, or other rolls that are based on that ability. It also determines how many of its linked [[Talents|E9-Talents]] are adjusted to 6+ or to 4+.

## The Six Abilities

### Strength

Your character's raw physical power, including their muscle strength and speed. It determines how well (and how hard) they can hit with melee weapons, how much they can lift and carry, and their general athleticism. Strength sets your melee attack throw, your save vs. [Hold](#hold), and your [carrying capacity](#carrying-capacity).

### Constitution

Your character's toughness, endurance, and health. It determines how long they can stay in a fight, as well as how well they resist poisons and diseases. Constitution sets your save vs. [Doom](#doom) and adds to your [hit die](#hit-die).

### Dexterity

Your character's hand-eye coordination, balance, and agility (although some facets of agility are a matter of strength rather than dexterity). It determines how well you can avoid getting hit, and gives you an edge with various trainable physical talents. Dexterity sets your ranged attack throw and your save vs. [Blast](#blast). Its talents are [Agility](E9-Talents#agility), [Finesse](E9-Talents#finesse) and [Stealth](E9-Talents#stealth).

### Intelligence

Your character's logical thinking and deduction capability. It determines how well you notice out-of-place details, and gives you an edge with various mental talents. Intelligence sets your save vs. [Trick](#trick). Its talents are [Lore](E9-Talents#lore), [Medicine](E9-Talents#medicine) and [Science](E9-Talents#science).

### Wisdom

Your character's intuitive awareness and common sense. It determines how well you resist various magical or mind-controlling effects, and gives you an edge with various trainable intuitive talents. Wisdom sets your save vs. [Curse](#curse). Its talents are [Alertness](E9-Talents#alertness), [Intuition](E9-Talents#intuition) and [Survival](E9-Talents#survival).

### Charisma

Your character's courage, spirit, and likeability. It determines how well you resist being frightened or discouraged, and gives you an edge with various social talents. Charisma sets your save vs. [Fear](#fear). Its talents are [Bearing](E9-Talents#bearing), [Charm](E9-Talents#charm) and [Wit](E9-Talents#wit).

## Saving Throws

Each ability has an associated saving throw. A saving throw is made by rolling a d20 and comparing it to a threshold: the number you need to match or beat on the d20 to successfully save vs. a threat of that nature. Each threshold starts at 14+ for a +0 ability and improves by 1 with each class level (see [[Advancement|E9-Advancement]]).

### Doom

**Constitution** vs. Doom: disease, poison, or trauma.

### Hold

**Strength** vs. Hold: knockdown, paralysis, or petrification.

### Blast

**Dexterity** vs. Blast: a cone, ray, or explosion.

### Trick

**Intelligence** vs. Trick: illusion or beguilement.

### Curse

**Wisdom** vs. Curse: a magic spell.

### Fear

**Charisma** vs. Fear: morale checks.

## Attack Throws

You also have two attack throws: one for ranged attacks, and one for melee attacks. Your **melee** attack throw threshold is based on your Strength, and your **ranged** attack throw threshold is based on your Dexterity. You must match or beat this value on a d20 roll to hit an enemy with that kind of attack. Fighters improve their attack throws as they level (see [Fighter Training](E9-Fighter#fighter-training)); other classes' attack throws depend only on their ability.

## Hit Die

Your hit die is used to determine your maximum hit points, as well as your rate of healing. Whenever you are asked to roll a hit die, you roll a d6 and add your Constitution; if you have a negative Constitution and your hit die total would be less than 1, treat the roll result as a 1 instead. Fighter classes gain a +2 bonus to their hit die.

## Carrying Capacity

Your character can only carry so much before the weight slows them down. Each character has a carrying capacity of **6 + their Strength**, and an encumbrance threshold of half that amount (round down). Carrying gear in excess of your encumbrance threshold halves your dash speed, up to a maximum practical burden equal to your carrying capacity. See [[Encumbrance|E9-Encumbrance]].

## Ability Checks

Sometimes, the result of an action will be determined purely by your character's ability, regardless of any training or experience they might have. An ability check rolls a d6 and adds the appropriate ability. (In a sense, hit dice are a kind of ability check.) The referee determines the threshold for success based on the circumstances.
