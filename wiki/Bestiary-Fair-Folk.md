## The Fair Folk

“The Fair Folk” is the traditional name for intelligent Fey creatures that can appear in a mortal guise, but whose natural forms are not made of Material substance. The Fair Folk are universally dangerous, although some can be successfully bargained with by those who are sufficiently careful and clever. Each Fair Folk is a unique creature, and most defy categorization -  although mortals are still wont to try. At best, the Fair Folk can be categorized by their apparent elemental correspondences, motivations, and modus operandi - although all of these are subject to change at the whim of the eldritch intelligences behind their fey masks.

The Fair Folk are fickle and treacherous, but they can be bound by geasa, and can bind mortals with geasa in turn. A Fair Folk that chooses to willingly bind itself by oath to treat peacefully with mortals is known in civilization as a Numen. Compared to an unbound Fair Folk, the demeanor of a Numen is surprisingly benevolent, and the longer a Numen and its mortal subjects remain true to their oaths, the more benevolent and humane its temperament becomes.

Mortal travelers are advised to be exceptionally wary of any Fair Folk they meet; they are known to delight in tricking mortals, and their words carry weight that can entrap even the cleverest of sages. The Church advises any mortal that has an unusual run-in with the fey to immediately flee, and report their experience to the nearest place of worship, where the local clergy will hopefully be better-equipped to handle the situation.

While the Fair Folk appear human, their bodies are merely constructs of Materia, woven out of the Elemental Chaos as they pass through towards the Material. As fey, their bodies are vulnerable to cold iron, which is any unalloyed iron dug up from the ground (not smelted from ore) that has never been heated enough to glow.

Damage to a fey creature from any cold iron weapon inflicts disadvantage on all saving throws until it heals. Any damage inflicted to a Fair Folk that is not from a cold iron weapon is only temporary; upon “death”, their essence simply flees back into the Elemental Chaos, where they weave themselves a new body and return again to vex the mortals that “vanquished” them.

A Fair Folk that takes an “interest” in a particular mortal is a recipe for legendary tales of woe and hardship. Their mercurial natures make them just as dangerous to “friend” and “foe” alike, and it is impossible to tell whether rescuing a Fair Folk or killing it is more likely to lead to that Fair Folk falling madly in love or swearing an undying oath of vengeance. Hence the Church’s stance to best let them be, hurry on one’s way, and tell the nearest Pastor.

Within Faerie, accepting any kind of help or generosity from the Fair Folk makes one vulnerable to both their beguiling glamours and their primal oath-weaving. Many tales end with a hapless mortal supping with an unknown stranger, only to find that they have been bound into service by a Fair Folk trickster looking for a new “pet”.

Numina are far more generous and benevolent with their gifts and their oaths, sanctifying promises made within their sacred groves and offering their bounty freely to all those who have honored their pacts. A Fair Folk who becomes a Numen might choose to release their mortal captives upon conversion, or those captives might find themselves permanently bound into service to  the new Numen - which will at least be substantially less unpleasant than “service” to an unbound Fair Folk master.

### Wisp

*(Level 1)*  

Wisps are tiny, barely-sentient bundles of materia that occasionally manifest within faerie. Nearly indestructible, a wisp that suffers any damage at all will dissipate, then re-form at the next sunrise or sunset. Wisps come in five elemental aspects – stone, water, wood, air, and flame. They are believed to be the “larval” form of other fair folk, eventually evolving into more powerful forms once they have gained enough experience to choose a coherent shape and personality.
```
Doom — Hold — Blast 12+ Trick 15+ Curse 14+ Fear 12+
Movement:      Fly             Size:  Tiny
Armor:         0               HP:    1
Attacks (12+): The wisp can make a ranged attack
               out to 2 paces which inflicts 1d6
               elemental damage
Con — Str —    Dex +2 Int -1   Wis +0 Cha +2
Talents (5+):  Alertness, Survival
```
**Primal Magic -** The wisp can cast a first-circle primal glamour of its element.

**Familiar Pact -** By swearing a mutual geas with a druid, a wisp can receive up to three glamour from that druid, allowing it to cast first-, second-, or third-circle elemental glamours of its element by spending glamour equal to the desired effect's circle. A wisp bound to a druid no longer has their own glamour, but must use whatever is granted them by their druid companion.

**Regeneration -** a Wisp reduced to 0 hit points does not die, but instead regenerates at the next sunrise or sunset, emerging out of the nearest natural occurrence of its element.

### Wisps and Druids

At the referee’s discretion, a druid harvesting materia from an environment might discover a wisp - a small piece of sentient materia that must be befriended or bargained with before it can be harvested and used. Elemental wisps typically manifest as small, sentient creatures made out of their element, which appear as tiny balls of ghostly light until they choose to reveal their full corporeal forms to a mortal. Wisps are capable of speech and locomotion, but are typically content to rest in a druid’s pockets once a pact of service is settled on. Wisps are fickle, so keeping them happy and willing to serve a druid can be challenging, but the rewards are often worth it - investing elemental glamour into a wisp allows that wisp to cast and maintain any glamour that matches its element, of any circle up to the amount of glamour invested into it, on behalf of the druid it has bonded with.

A first level druid may invest one glamour into a wisp; a third level druid may invest up to two glamour into a single wisp, and a fifth level druid may invest up to three glamour into any one wisp. A wisp may use this glamour to cast any elemental effect that matches its own element, and any glamour cast by a wisp is sustained by that wisp rather than the druid.

In addition to its spellcasting aid, a wisp provides a druid with something akin to a wizard’s cantrips - small, at-will magics related to their elemental aspect. A flame wisp might provide a spark to light a campfire, an air wisp might accurately predict today’s weather for a druid, or a water wisp might help a druid resist being poisoned. Each wisp can also provide a simple elemental attack out to 5 paces, which uses the druid’s ranged attack throw and inflicts 1d6 damage on a hit. Wisps are hardy little creatures, which can regenerate their bodies from the tiniest spark of elemental energy, and retreat into Faerie when that body is damaged or destroyed. A wisp that is hurt too often in the service of a druid will likely abandon that druid, but otherwise they are nigh indestructible companions.

### Pixie

*(Level 2)*  

Pixies appear to be diminutive elves with beautiful, diaphanous insect wings that sparkle in iridescent colors. All pixies are natural wellsprings of primal fey magic – a newborn pixie is able to cast primal spells almost as quickly as a newborn foal is able to take its first steps. Pixies tend to see their magic as a natural extension of themselves, and thus do not usually think to use tools or technology the way the “big folk” do.  A pixie is typically too small to wield a weapon larger than a sewing needle.
```
Doom 13+ Hold 13+ Blast 10+ Trick 13+ Curse 11+ Fear 11+
Movement:      Slow, Fly       Size:  Tiny
Armor:         0               HP:    3
Attacks (10+): The pixie can make a ranged attack
               out to 5 paces which inflicts 2d6
               psychic, aetheric, or elemental damage
               of its choice.
Con +0 Str +0  Dex +3 Int +0   Wis +2 Cha +2
Talents (4+):  Finesse, Stealth, Survival, Charm, Wit
```
**Primal Magic -** As a druid.

### Boggle

*(Level 3)*  

In their natural form, boggles appear to be small, twisted grey goblins with oversized heads, eyes, and hands. They frequently change shape to attempt to frighten away interlopers, taking on the form and apparent behavior of any living thing that they have observed long enough to mimic.
```
Doom 12+ Hold 12+ Blast 12+ Trick 12+ Curse 12+ Fear 12+
Movement:      Normal          Size: Small
Armor:         0               HP:  (3d6)÷2
Attacks (8+):  The boggle can make eye contact
               with a target to make a ranged attack
               out to 5 paces which inflicts 1d6
               psychic damage and frightens the
               target unless they save.
Con +0 Str +0  Dex +0 Int +0   Wis +0 Cha +0
Talents (4+):  Finesse, Stealth, Intuition, Survival, Wit
```
**Shapeshift -** The boggle has three glamour. It can spend one glamour as an exploration action to change its appearance and voice to match any other small, medium, or large creature it has closely observed for one previous exploration turn.

### Goodfellow

*(Level 4)*  

Goodfellows are Fair Folk that wander deserted highways and unbroken earth, pretending to be mortal travelers. They may appear as any kind of mortal imaginable, but often appear as an elf, hob, or goblin. A goodfellow will often have a particular unusual feature that might reveal their fey nature to a perceptive mortal - such as backward-facing hands or feet, eyes that don’t reflect the light as they should, or hair that blows the wrong way in the wind.

A goodfellow that is spotted for what it is will typically immediately promise aid of some kind to the “clever mortal” that saw through its disguise, in exchange for a promise in kind to never tell another mortal soul about its true nature. Keeping this promise will usually “reward” the mortal with an even mixture of aid and mischief, while breaking the promise - even accidentally - will curse the mortal with no end of bad luck.
```
Doom 11+ Hold 11+ Blast 11+ Trick 11+ Curse 9+ Fear 9+
Movement:      Normal         Size:   Medium
Armor:         1 (0)          HP:     4d6
Attacks (10+): feysilver rapier (1d6 piercing)
Con +0 Str +0  Dex +0 Int +0  Wis +2 Cha +2
Talents (4+):  Finesse, Lore, Survival, Charm, Wit
```
**Primal Magic -** As a druid.

**Geas -**  Once per encounter as its action, the goodfellow can spend three glamour to cause any promise made or implied to become a sworn geas, binding the speaker to its word.

### Thyrs

*(Level 5)*  

Thyrsa are a form of stone troll that form barbarian tribes across the wilds of Europa. All thyrsa have an innate affinity for stone and an aversion to sunlight, suffering 2d6 petrification damage per round when in bright sunlight, or 1d6 damage per round when in dim or diffuse sunlight.

Thyrsa are intelligent creatures, and often have the champion, hunter, or druid class template. While often solitary, more sociable trolls occasionally gather together in family dwellings, or even establish small villages.
```
Doom 6+ Hold 6+ Blast 12+ Trick 10+ Curse 9+ Fear 10+
Movement:      Normal          Size:  Large
Armor:         2               HP:    (5d6+20)x2
Attacks (5+):  per wielded weapon; as a Large creature,
               a thyrs’s weapon attacks deal 2x damage.
Con +4 Str +4  Dex -2  Int +0  Wis +1 Cha +0
Talents (3+):  Science, Survival, Medicine
```
**Regenerate -** The thyrs may spend one glamour to roll a hit die, then recover that many hit points.

**Stoneheart -** Thyrsa lose their regeneration ability until the next sunrise or sunset when exposed to sunlight, which inflicts 2d6 damage per round until the troll is turned to stone. At the next sunset, a troll that has been turned to stone immediately un-petrifies and rolls all its hit dice to heal.

### Hag

*(Level 6)*  

Hags are fair folk nightmares that embody mortals’ fear of strangers and sorcery. In their natural form, a hag appears to be a hideously ugly, impossibly old woman with a maw full of razor-sharp teeth. However, most hags will cast illusions over themselves and their surroundings, most often in order to appear as kindly old women living in warm, inviting cottages.

Hags collect all manner of magical trinkets, regardless of their use to the hag, but items that aid in weaving illusions are especially coveted. They also delight in enslaving mortals, and turning them to cannibalism and depravity. A hag will occasionally “adopt” a goblin orc tribe, granting them magical protection in exchange for any shiny baubles they can bring back from their raids.
```
Doom 7+ Hold 7+ Blast 7+ Trick 7+ Curse 7+ Fear 7+
Con +1 (7+)    Str +1  (7+)   Dex +1  (7+)
Int +1 (7+)    Wis +1  (7+)   Cha +1  (7+)
Movement:      Normal         Size:   Medium
Armor:         0              HP:     6d6+6
Attacks (8+):  Evil Eye (10 pace ranged attack,
               2d6 psychic stunning damage).
Con +1 Str +1  Dex +1  Int +1 Wis +1  Cha +1
Talents (3+):  Survival, Intuition, Medicine, Wit
```
**Primal Magic -**As a druid.

## Nymphs

Nymphs are fey spirits that embody the primal Material elements - stone, water, wood, air, and flame. While most Fair Folk move freely between the Aether and the Material, nymphs choose to anchor themselves into the Material and Faerie, giving up some freedom in exchange for a deeper connection to the playground of the mortal world.

Nymphs invariably manifest as beautiful men or women carved from living materia, with features taken from the specific stone, water, tree, ravine, or hearth they are bound to. A nymph numen will become a fierce protector of the mortals under its care, so long as those mortals are equally fierce protectors of its tree and the surrounding lands.

When a druid casts the Nature’s Ally spell without specifying a creature for their wisp familiar to transform into, the wisp will almost always manifest as a nymph of the appropriate element. This tendency, plus the marked resemblance of most  wisps to “baby”

nymphs, lends credence to the theory that wisps are somehow the ‘larval’ forms of nymphs. Since a wisp’s material anchor is their druid, a wisp-nymph’s magics must be fed by their druid’s primal attunements, rather than their own innate glamour.

### Oreiad

*(Level 7)*  

Oreiades are nymphs that bind themselves to the earth itself, usually to a particular large boulder, vein of crystal, or precious metal.
```
Doom 5+ Hold 7+  Blast 8+  Trick 8+  Curse 5+ Fear 5+
Movement:      Normal         Size:   Medium
Armor:         3              HP:          7d6+9 + 2SP
Attacks (7+):  obsidian shards (1d6 piercing)
Con +3 Str +1  Dex +0 Int +0  Wis +3 Cha +3
Talents (3+):  Intuition, Survival, Charm
```
**Primal Magic -** The oreiad may cast any Stone- or Fey-aspected glamour by spending glamour equal to its circle.

**Recharge -** As its action, the oreiad may regain one primal attunement if it is touching solid unbroken earth or natural stone or bedrock.

**Regenerate -** As its action, the oreiad rolls 1d6+3 and heals that many hit points, or one structure point if it rolls 10 or higher. The oreiad must be on natural stone or bedrock to perform this action.

### Naiad

*(Level 7)*  

Like all nymphs, Naiads are fey spirits that choose to bind themselves to living matter. A naiad binds themselves to a river, brook, lake, or other body of water.

Naiad numina are fiercely loyal to their mortal subjects, so long as the health and purity of their waters are protected. A naiad numen whose water becomes polluted rapidly becomes corrupted and evil, delighting both in wanton destruction and minor acts of sadistic cruelty.

Ocean naiad, called nereid, bind themselves to a particular bay or inlet or other piece of coastland. They are less likely to become numen, but those that do are unparalleled protectors of the sailors within sight of their shores.
```
Doom 7+ Hold 7+  Blast 5+  Trick 8+  Curse 5+ Fear 5+
Movement:      Swim 20         Size:  Medium
Armor:         0               HP:    7d6+7
Attacks (7+):  watery grapple (1d6 bashing)
Con +1 Str +1  Dex +3 Int +0   Wis +3 Cha +3
Talents (3+):  Intuition, Survival, Wit
```
**Primal Magic -** The naiad may cast any Water- or Fey-aspected glamour by spending glamour equal to its circle.

**Recharge -** As its action, the naiad may regain one primal attunement if it is touching its body of water.

**Regenerate -** As its action, the naiad rolls 1d6+1 and heals that many hit points. The naiad must be within 100’ of its body of water to perform this action.

### Dryad

*(Level 7)*  

Dryads are nymphs that choose to bind themselves to a single living tree. Eventually, their fey nature takes on enough aspects of the tree that its survival and health become their greatest passion.

Dryad numen are particularly benevolent, working to ensure bountiful harvests for their mortal subjects in exchange for protection from fire, disease, and drought. In exchange, any damage to their sacred tree - whether intentional or not - must be met with swift and cruel punishment, meted out within the dryad’s sacred grove.
```
Doom 6+ Hold 7+  Blast 6+  Trick 8+  Curse 5+ Fear 5+
Movement:      Normal         Size:   Medium
Armor:         1              HP:     7d6+14
Attacks (7+):  thorn rake (1d6 poison)
Con +2 Str +1  Dex +2 Int +0  Wis +3 Cha +3
Talents (3+):  Intuition, Survival, Medicine, Charm
```
**Primal Magic -** The dryad may cast any Wood- or Fey-aspected glamour by spending glamour equal to its circle.

**Recharge -** As its action, the dryad may regain one primal attunement if it is touching its tree. **Regenerate -** As its action, the dryad rolls 1d6+2 and heals that many hit points. The dryad must be within 100’ of its tree to perform this action.

### Auriad

*(Level 7)*  

Unlike other nymphs, Auriads choose to remain unbound from a single piece of base matter, embodying the freedom and capriciousness of the winds themselves.

Auriads almost never become numina, finding the binding nature of oaths inimical to their very natures.
```
Doom 8+ Hold 8+  Blast 6+  Trick 8+  Curse 5+ Fear 5+
Con +0 (8+)    Str +0  (8+)    Dex +2 (6+)
Int  +0 (8+)   Wis +3  (5+)    Cha +3 (5+)
Movement:      Fly 20          Size: Medium
Armor:         0               HP: 7d6
Attacks:       resonant scream (1d6 thunder)
Con +0 Str +0  Dex +2 Int +0   Wis +3 Cha +3
Talents (3+):  Agility, Stealth, Science, Lore,
               Intuition, Survival, Wit
```
**Primal Magic -** The auriad may cast any Airor Fey-aspected primal glamour by spending glamour equal to its circle.

**Recharge -** As its action, the auriad may regain one primal attunement if it is unrestrained and flying under the open sky.

**Regenerate -** As its action, the dryad rolls 1d6 and heals that many hit points. The auriad must be unrestrained and flying under the open sky.

### Hestaiad

*(Level 7)*  

Hestaiads are nymphs who choose to incarnate as sacred hearth-flames and household guardians. Most hestaiads are drawn to peaceful households and sacred sites, and offer themselves up as numina almost immediately upon first manifesting a material body.

Those hestaiads who remain wild fair folk are terrifying elemental forces of rage and wildfire, eager to consume and burn down all that they see. Once they have devoured everything burnable, a wild hestaiad will typically gutter and “die”, returning to the Elemental Chaos until a new opportunity to burn and consume presents itself.
```
Doom 7+ Hold 7+  Blast 5+  Trick 8+  Curse 5+ Fear 5+
Con +1 (7+)    Str +1  (7+)   Dex +3  (5+)
Int  +0 (8+)   Wis +3  (5+)   Cha +3  (5+)
Movement:      Dash 25        Size:   Medium
Armor:         0              HP:     7d6+7
Attacks:       burning touch (1d6 fire)
Con +1 Str +1  Dex +3 Int +0  Wis +3 Cha +3
Talents (3+):  Intuition, Survival, Medicine, Charm
```
**Primal Magic -** The hestaiad may cast any Flame- or Fey-aspected primal glamour by spending glamour equal to its circle.

**Recharge -** As its action, the hestaiad may regain one primal attunement if it is within its hearth.

**Regenerate -** As its action, the hestaiad rolls 1d6+1 and heals that many hit points. The hestaiad must be within its hearth to perform this action.

### Leannán-Sí

*(Level 8)*  

The Leannán-Sí is an achingly beautiful Fair Folk seductress, who promises eternal love to a mortal in exchange for their vitality and lifeforce. A Leannán-Sí is not necessarily female; the fey spirit will reach into the mind of a vulnerable mortal, and weave itself a body that most perfectly matches that mortal’s desires. While it survives in this form, that mortal’s saving throws vs. Trick from the Leannán-Sí are harder.
```
Doom 8+ Hold 8+  Blast 5+  Trick 6+  Curse 6+ Fear 5+
Movement:      Normal          Size:  Medium
Armor:         0               HP:    8d6-16
Attacks:       none, but can cast beguile at-will.
Con -2 Str -2  Dex +2 Int +1   Wis +1 Cha +5
Talents (2+):  Finesse, Intuition, Charm, Wit
```
**Primal Magic -** The leannan-si has ten glamour, which it can spend to cast primal effects. Casting a primal glamour costs a number of glamour equal to its circle.

**Fey Seductress -** All of the Leanan-Sí’s beguiling effects are harder to resist.

**Drain Essence -** As its action, the Leanan-Sí may touch a willing mortal, inflicting a critical hit as the mortal visibly ages 1d6 years. The Leanan-Sí then regains one glamour.

A chosen mortal who swears their undying love to the Leannán-Sí receives its earnest and undying love in return, and will be genuinely cherished and protected as it gradually drains their life-force.

### Horned Hunter

*(Level 9)*  

The Horned Hunter is an impossibly tall, intimidating Fair Folk hunter with elk antlers growing from its head, wearing hunting-garb woven from forest leaves. It often rides a Nightmare steed, and is surrounded by fey spirits that take the guise of Dire Wolves, which serve as its hunting-hounds.
```
Doom 5+ Hold 5+  Blast 5+  Trick 8+  Curse 5+ Fear 5+
Movement:      Very Fast      Size:   Large
Armor:         2              HP:     (9d6+36)x2
Attacks (5+ x2): Legendary Aetheric Blade dagger
               (1d6+Str+4 piercing sneak attack),
               Legendary Windbow greatbow
               (2d6+4 piercing and poison sneak attack)
Con +4 Str +4  Dex +4 Int +1  Wis +4 Cha +0
Talents (2+):  Agility, Stealth, Intuition,
               Alertness, Survival, Bearing
```
**Primal Hunter -** The horned hunter has ten glamour, which it can spend to cast elemental primal effects. Casting a primal glamour costs a number of glamour equal to its circle.

**Wild Hunt -** As its action, the Horned Hunter may designate a quarry for its hunt. While it maintains focus, it unerringly knows the distance and direction to its quarry, regardless of any natural or supernatural means of concealment or misdirection, and it can always recognize its quarry despite any disguise or illusion.
