There are 12 talents, each linked to an ability. Each talent determines the threshold for various d6 **task checks**, which are made by rolling a d6 and comparing it to that talent's threshold. All character classes use talents, but [experts](E9-Expert) especially rely on them.

## Starting Thresholds

Your Dexterity, Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma each set the starting threshold for three talents. Talents usually begin at a threshold of **5+**, but if the linked ability is positive, that many of its talents begin at **4+** instead; if the ability is negative, that many begin at **6+** (see the [ability table](E9-Abilities#ability-table)). You may choose which talents get the better or worse starting threshold for each ability, or roll a d6 to assign them randomly.

## Training

When a talent is trained, its threshold improves by 1, to a minimum threshold of **2+**. Experts train a talent of their choice at every class level, including level 1; magic-users train specific talents at even levels; fighters can train certain talents as a feat. See [[Advancement|E9-Advancement]].

### Agility

*Dexterity.* Your full-body athleticism: to climb surfaces, maintain balance, and perform precise athletic actions. Agility checks are harder if you are wearing medium or heavy armor or a shield, or if you are encumbered.

### Finesse

*Dexterity.* Your hand-eye coordination, used for detailed work: to pick locks, disarm traps, operate machinery, assemble a clockwork contraption, or craft or repair items.

### Stealth

*Dexterity.* Your ability to move your body within an environment without making noise or being seen: to move silently, hide from view, surprise or ambush an enemy, or pick pockets. Stealth checks are harder if you are wearing medium or heavy armor or a shield, or if you are encumbered.

### Lore

*Intelligence.* Your linguistic ability, "book learning" and capacity to remember trivia: to understand a language, decipher a code, or recall any knowledge involving history, religion, or culture.

### Medicine

*Intelligence.* Your understanding of living bodies and how to maintain or restore their health: to brew an elixir or toxin, or to diagnose or treat injury or disease.

### Science

*Intelligence.* Your grasp of physical and magical phenomena and the rules that govern them: to brew a potion, enchant or identify a scroll or magic item, notice the presence of supernatural forces, or recall any knowledge involving math or engineering.

### Alertness

*Wisdom.* Your ability to pick up details and clues from your environment, determine which ones are important, and know how to respond to them: to detect a hidden trap or secret door, to listen or watch for enemies, and to avoid being surprised by ambushes.

### Intuition

*Wisdom.* Your "common sense" and how well you can listen to your gut, both in social situations and in more unusual circumstances: to read the emotions and intentions of living creatures, or to get a "gut sense" of danger or extreme foolishness.

### Survival

*Wisdom.* Your ability to navigate the wilds and survive within them: to navigate the wilderness, hunt or forage for food, follow others' tracks or hide your own, or set up camp, including finding or creating shelter, food, water, and other necessities.

### Bearing

*Charisma.* Your ability to project authority, command respect, and maintain your composure in tense situations: to maintain composure, manage hirelings and followers, or improve your allies' morale. Bearing also limits how many [followers](E9-Social-Encounters#recruiting-followers) you can lead.

### Charm

*Charisma.* Your ability to project trust, warmth, or anything else that could influence another creature's long-term attitude towards you: to sway another intelligent creature's attitude. See [Influencing NPCs](E9-Social-Encounters#influencing-npcs).

### Wit

*Charisma.* Your ability to be persuasive, whether you are trying to change another creature's beliefs or just convince them to perform some task or action: to influence another intelligent creature's immediate behavior or beliefs. Wit checks are harder if that creature's current attitude towards you is unfriendly, or easier if its attitude is friendly.
