## Crowns

A crown is any magic item that is worn on the head or upper face, such as a hat or spectacles.

### Crown of Command

*Rare (Arcane)*  

*Ingredients: Dream Pearl, orichalc, gold*

A Crown of Command appears to be a finely made golden crown with a polished red gem.

On closer inspection, the inside of the crown contains tiny sigils and runes, filled with a metal of a slightly different color.

The wearer may use their action to cast the friends cantrip at-will, or to cast the beguile, suggestion, or dominate spell from the crown. If beguile is cast, the crown may be used again ten minutes after the spell is released; if suggestion is cast, the crown requires one hour to recharge; if dominate is cast, it recharges at the next sunrise or sunset.

### Darkvision Goggles

*Uncommon (Arcane)*  

*Ingredients: Flame Pearl, orichalc, gold*

Darkvision goggles appear to be finely made gold spectacles with faintly glowing lenses. On closer inspection, the lenses and frames contain tiny sigils and runes, filled with a bright gold metal.

Once activated, the wearer of these spectacles can see 10 paces in total darkness for 1d6 turns. While completely undetectable to mundane sight, while active the goggles appear as a bright beacon to anything that can see magic. The spectacles recharge after the next sunrise or sunset.

### Diadem of Splendour

*Uncommon (Arcane)*  

*Ingredients: Dream Pearl, feysilver, silver*

A diadem of splendour appears to be a finely made silver crown with a polished green gem. The inside of the crown contains tiny sigils and runes, filled with a metal of a slightly different color.

The wearer gains a bonus to their Bearing based on the crown’s rarity: +1 for an uncommon crown, +2 for a rare crown, or +3 for a legendary crown. If another magical effect also increases the wearer’s Bearing, only the highest effect takes hold.

### Golem Eye

*Uncommon+ (Arcane)*  

*Ingredients: ironwood, devil creeper, jade,*

*hippogriff leather, Wood Pearl*

A golem eye is an artificial sensor, designed to replace a maimed or missing eye. One or both eyes may be replaced as a single uncommon item, which operates nearly identically to the wearer’s original eyes. A rare or legendary prosthesis will have some arcane divination effect - usually identical to the enchantment in a ‘goggles’ or ‘spectacles’ type magic item - incorporated into its design.

### Puppeteer’s Crowns

*Rare (Arcane)*  

*Ingredients: Two Dream Pearls, orichalc, feysilver,*

*gold, silver*

Puppeteer’s crowns appear to be a pair of finely made royal circlets, one silver and one gold, each inlaid with opal and mother-ofpearl. On closer inspection, the inside of each crown contains tiny sigils and runes, filled with a metal of a slightly different color.

The wearer of the gold crown uses their action to place the silver crown on a willing or restrained creature’s head, where it immediately magically resizes to fit them and fades from view. Once both creatures are wearing their crowns, the wearer of the silver crown makes a saving throw vs. curses. If they succeed, both crowns fall off, and neither may be used until the next sunrise or sunset. If the wearer of the silver crown fails their saving throw, the wearer of the golden crown may direct their actions and perceive through their senses for as long as they maintain focus. Any damage that either creature receives is immediately also inflicted on the other wearer, forcing a save vs. Doom to maintain focus. Once the effect ends, both crowns fall off and can’t be used again until after the next sunrise or sunset.

### Radiant Diadem

*Common (Arcane)*  

*Ingredients: Flame Pearl, feysilver, silver*

A radiant diadem appears to be a thin silver circlet with a highly polished silver disc at the forehead, upon which is mounted a flat, clear stone. On closer inspection, the back of the disc and the metal frame contain tiny sigils and runes, filled with a darker silvery metal. When activated, the crown emits bright light in a cone out to 5 paces, and dim light out to 10 paces.

### Spectacles of Arcane Sight

*Uncommon (Arcane)*  

*Ingredients: Aether Pearl, feysilver, silver*

Spectacles of arcane sight appear to be finely made silver spectacles with faintly darkened lenses. On closer inspection, the lenses and frames contain tiny sigils and runes, filled with a dark silvery metal.

The wearer of these spectacles gains the detect magic cantrip at-will, and may use the spectacles' charge to cast identify magic or arcane sight.

Roll 1d6 to determine the number of turns each effect lasts; once used, the spectacles require one hour to recharge after casting identify magic, or one sunrise or sunset to recharge after casting arcane sight.

### Spectacles of Distant Vision

*Rare (Arcane)*  

*Ingredients: Flame Pearl, orichalc, gold*

Spectacles of distant vision appear to be finely made gold spectacles with faintly glowing lenses, and a clear gemstone affixed between them. On closer inspection, the lenses and frames contain tiny sigils and runes, filled with a bright gold metal.

The wearer of these spectacles may use them to cast the arcane eye spell. Once used, these spectacles recharge after the next sunrise or sunset.

### Spectacles of True Seeing

*Rare (Arcane)*  

*Ingredients: Dream Pearl, feysilver, silver*

Spectacles of true seeing appear to be finely made silver spectacles with a slight opalescent sheen to the lenses. On closer inspection, the lenses and frames contain tiny sigils and runes, filled with a dark silvery metal.

The wearer of these spectacles finds all saving throws to disbelieve illusion effects easier, and can attempt to disbelieve an illusion once per combat round on their turn without using their action.

As their action, the wearer can immediately see one creature or object as it truly is, piercing all illusions placed upon it. If the object or creature is under the effect of some kind of transforming magic, the wearer can see both its current true form, and what its true form would be without that magic. Once used in this manner, the spectacles recharge on the next sunrise or sunset.

### Stone of Aioun

*Legendary (Arcane)*  

*Ingredients: Aether Pearl, feysilver*

An aioun stone is a legendary Atlantean aether pearl the size of a pea, woven with almost imperceptibly thin feysilver circuitry. A wizard in possession of an aioun stone may prepare one additional arcane spell of any school and circle into the stone when they prepare their spells at sunrise or sunset. Once the spell is prepared into the stone, it begins glowing softly and floats into the air, orbiting rapidly around the wearer’s head.

Once the spell is cast, the stone begins glowing brightly, and may be used to sustain the spell without focus if the spell normally requires focus. After the spell is finished, the stone falls inert, and may not hold another spell until the next sunrise or sunset.
