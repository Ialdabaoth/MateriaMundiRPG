### Blind

A creature can be blinded by various circumstances. A blind creature considers all other creatures effectively [concealed](#concealed) from it. A creature might sometimes be able to end this condition with a successful save vs. Doom.

### Concealed

A creature can be partially or totally concealed by cover, darkness, smoke or fog, or other circumstances. A partially concealed creature is harder to hit, while a fully concealed creature cannot be attacked unless the attacker has some way of knowing where they are. A creature totally concealed by hard cover usually can't be attacked at all.

### Dying

A creature with zero hp is dying. A dying creature is also [unconscious](#unconscious), and whenever it suffers further damage, it must save vs. Doom or die.

At the end of each round, after all other creatures have acted, each dying creature must save vs. Doom or die. If its saving throw rolls a 20, it stabilizes to one hit point and is no longer dying, but it's still unconscious and helpless until it wakes up.

Another creature can attempt to stabilize a dying creature with a hard [Medicine](E9-Talents#medicine) check. On a success, the dying creature stabilizes to one hit point and is no longer dying, but it's still unconscious and helpless until it wakes up.

### Hobbled

Various attacks or other circumstances might hobble a creature. A hobbled creature can only move 3 paces per round, and can't dash or evade. A creature might sometimes be able to end this condition with a successful save vs. Hold.

### Prone

Being knocked prone is similar to being [hobbled](#hobbled), but a prone creature can stand up during their next action unless they're somehow restrained from doing so.

### Injured

A creature that has lost half of its hit points is injured, and will need either magic, or time and medical attention, in order to heal. See [Injury](E9-Resting-and-Healing#injury).

### Panicked

A panicked creature can't attack, and must attempt to move as far away from the source of its fear as it can (dashing if possible). If it can't move, it cowers in place as far away as it can get.

### Stunned

A stunned creature loses its action for the rest of the combat round, causing it to be unable to act, react, or move. However, it isn't considered helpless.

### Helpless

A helpless creature can't move, act, or react, and all attacks against it are easy.

### Unconscious

An unconscious creature is [helpless](#helpless), and also completely unaware of its surroundings. At the end of the round, any unconscious creature that isn't dying makes an [Alertness](E9-Talents#alertness) check to attempt to wake up.
